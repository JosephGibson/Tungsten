# Example 01: platformer

A wizard with a staff light crosses a ruin at night: six areas from the lantern clearing to the troll bridge, each flowing into the next, with lava, moving decks and lifts on the way and a ball pit dug into the clearing under a rope bridge. Ten spells cast physics toys and the elements: a fire missile, a flamethrower, an ice spray, lightning, a force push, a black hole, iron blocks, a bowling ball, a stream of small balls and a summons, the training dummy, an enemy, a crate or a keg; things burn, freeze, explode, shatter, burst into steam, conduct, crush and fall into the pit. Pine crates catch fire and fall in, and red powder kegs blow up on a fuse and set each other off. A gun fires beside the spells, and a training dummy in the clearing takes every element and shows its damage. Six enemies hunt the wizard: a fire wisp, a frost crawler, a rust beetle, a cave bat, a cave troll and a goblin sapper, which drop health and mana orbs.

It is a game on the [template](../../templates/basic/README.md): the template's shell (title, pause, game over, saves, settings, keybindings, toasts) copied byte for byte into `src/shell/` and its theme's structure into `assets/ui/themes/shell.json` in 01's own colours, darker backgrounds and a vivid purple, the seam in `src/game.rs` and `src/save.rs`, and the game in `src/features/`, built on the engine kit (`tungsten::kit`) wherever the kit has the mechanic. Its night is a bloom and a vignette that each run builds whole (`features/level/`); the shell's Bloom option, on the Display tab, takes the bloom out and leaves the vignette. While no run is live, the title shows the night in OLED black, looking up past the treeline, with stars twinkling and a shooting star every few seconds (`features/level/title.rs`).

## Run and test

Run it from this folder, where `tungsten.json`, `input.json` and `assets/` sit:

```bash
cd examples/01_platformer
cargo run     # a debug build, with hot reload of assets/ and input.json; F4 shows the engine's HUD, F1 the colliders with the kit's debug shapes and each blow's box while it lands
cargo test    # tests/game/ on the engine's headless harness
```

From the repository root, `cargo test -p example-01-platformer` runs the same tests and `just smoke` starts the game from this folder. The art, the level, the sounds and the prefabs and data are generated by `tools/`, which [tools/README.md](tools/README.md) describes; `just level-check` checks them.

The map is 430 × 72 tiles of 64 px, generated from `tools/level.json` ([tools/README.md](tools/README.md)). Its six areas run west to east, each with its own ground, art, light, ambience loop, banner and autosave:

Each area flows into the next through a border of mixed tiles, and its look crossfades over the five tiles either side as the view crosses it. Outdoors, each area draws its own far strip between the ridges and the woodland, the ravine's and the mine's far walls standing dark behind their mist; the ravine, the mine and the bridge sit under a true-black night sky without the woodland, while the clearing and the aqueduct keep the moonlit one. Inside the hollow the light dims and the moon goes out, eased over a second, and every lit sprite darkens with it; the wisps, the fires and the staff light the cave. Each area's ambience loop rises over 1.5 s as the wizard enters, the others falling silent, and every loop is silent under pause and game over. Lava is a surface the wizard never sinks below: its touch burns and throws the wizard up and toward the nearer bank, an enemy that falls through it too, and it never goes out, though the ice spray sets a basin's crust solid for six seconds, its cracks glowing before it melts: as the chill builds, one front of crust comes down from the surface through the whole basin, frost mist rising off it, and it sets with a hiss; its cracks snap and spark at each of their three stages, and a second front comes down as it melts, glugging and spattering. Mist rises off the falls' plunge, billowing as it climbs. Shots meet the walls by their material: iron turns bullets and the missile, wood lets bullets through, and stone and the tiles end them.

The fire missile flies as a rocket of fire. The sprays draw as animated flames and frost: a flare at the staff, a stream of puffs that pile up where their lines meet something, a splash there and, for the ice, a bright core beam; whatever the cone damages throws off embers or ice crystals every 0.2 s, so a hit shows. The wizard casts each spell with its gesture: a bolt of the staff for the missile and lightning, a shove of the hand for the push and the hole, a conjuring strike for iron and the summons, a toss for the bowling ball, and the staff levelled along the aim while a spray or the small balls pour; the gun raises his pistol.

A refused cast says why and spends nothing: a sound for no mana, a tick for a spell not ready, and a fizzle where nothing fits. A jump under small balls shoves them off the wizard and rises clear. He walks bodies up to a keg's weight aside, slowed by their share of his own, while the dummy and iron stop him; walkers step over the balls, rocks and bombs in their way and shove them, never a crate or a keg.

The gun fires nine rounds 0.2 s apart, all back 1.2 s after the last or after R on a magazine short of nine, the trigger clicking while it reloads or runs dry: a shot deals 6 physical to what it hits, which can crit, kicks a ball and ends, shatters a frozen ball, and turns off iron twice, an iron block or the mine's iron faces, with sparks; it passes the mine's wooden scaffolds and the rope bridge in a burst of splinters and ends at stone in a puff of dust.

Mana starts at 100 of 100 and comes back at 30 a second, paused for a second after each cast. The staff light, the amber orb on the wizard's staff, is off at a new run's start and lit with L; it burns 3 mana a second while lit, which the regeneration covers at full mana; with none left it gutters out in a puff of smoke and relights at 10. The HUD shows the health and mana bars at the top left, each filled with its own liquid flowing by the game clock, health's pulsing and its frame beating red at a third or less, the pact's shield a light blue liquid over health's from the left, as wide as its share of the maximum, with its "+50" after the health's numbers, and under the bars the tomes held, a tile each in the order taken; and the spell bar at the bottom: each slot's icon tile, framed in its element's colour, with its key as bound on a corner plate (a rebind shows at once), its cooldown's sweep and its charges, the selected slot ringed and a spell the mana cannot pay for greyed; a slot flashes white as a cooldown of half a second or more ends, and red when a press finds it not ready. Slot 0 shows the summon's type, and its name over it a moment after ↓ or ↑ turns it. Set apart past a divider, the gun shows its rounds as cartridges with their count under them, and "reloading", its tile greyed under the reload's sweep and every cartridge spent, through any reload, an R reload with rounds left too.

## The elements

Every hit is typed: `damage.physical` (with its bleed), `damage.fire`, `damage.cold` and `damage.shock`, each in its colour. A hit from the wizard crits one time in twenty for twice its damage. What has health climbs each type's ladder: physical bleeds flesh and shatters the frozen, fire burns, cold chills and then freezes what is freezable, and shock leaves its target taking half again from every hit, then stuns it. Balls, iron and scraps keep their own states: small balls burn for ten seconds, and cold freezes balls and iron for five. A blow on flesh or the wizard spills blood, and the wizard reddens and reels for a moment; cold frosts a creature that freezes from its first point, white rime climbing in four stages from its feet to a crust with finer flakes each stage, and the chill slows it more as it builds toward the freeze; a frozen thing's ice cracks and flickers before it thaws.

A frozen thing ends one of three ways: fire on it explodes it (thermal shock), a hard impact or a fall shatters it, and cold on a burning thing bursts into steam, which puts out what burns around it. Explosions chain through a frozen pile and steam through a burning one, at most 48 a step, so a chain rolls through a pile as a wave, each ending credited to whoever began it. Moving bodies hurt what they hit by their speed and mass, a body under 4 by half its mass, and a hit worth less than 2 lands nothing.

Crates and kegs are bodies like iron, pushed, pulled, chilled, frozen and shattered as it is. A pine crate catches from fire, a burning ball or crate, a missile's heat or a blast, burns for eight seconds, lighting the crates and kegs beside it after two and the small balls touching it at once, and falls in; a burning crate thrown into a creature burns it. A bullet passes a crate in splinters, the troll lifts and throws one, and cold, steam and a black hole put one out, its fire kept for later, and keep it from catching for four seconds. Whatever lights a crate lights a powder keg's fuse, which blows it after 1.5 s; a bullet, lightning, a blast or a hard blow blows it at once: a fall of more than about 150 px, or a body of a bowling ball's weight or more meeting it as fast, a lighter one only faster, and a small ball or a goblin's bomb never. A keg's blast burns and batters what is within three tiles, lights the crates and sets off the kegs in its reach and explodes what is frozen there, credited to whoever lit the first; a frozen keg never lights, and fire on it shatters it. A goblin's bomb rolls where it lands, its fuse turning with it, and a goblin's rock stays a stone that bounces, rolls and can be pushed, pulled and thrown about, 24 at most, the oldest crumbling. Damage numbers float in each type's colour, a type's ticks merged into one and a crit at once, larger and yellow, while the Damage numbers option is on. Lava takes whatever falls into it: a ball, iron, a scrap, a crate or a rock goes in steam and embers with a sizzle, a keg or a goblin's bomb goes off, and a frozen crate or keg first shatters in the heat.

## The enemies

Each patrols, sees the wizard along a line that no tile, slab, crate, keg, iron, dummy or frozen enemy blocks, stops for its tell when it does and alerts the enemies within 320 px of it, then chases, attacks after a tell, searches where it last saw the wizard and goes home; one past 1,200 px from the wizard thinks nothing. A fresh alert shows "!" over the enemy with one sting for all it alerts, and a search "?". The map places twenty-six: a goblin on the clearing's meadow, three crawlers, two bats and a goblin in the aqueduct, five bats and the falls' crawler in the ravine, three wisps in the hollow, four beetles and three goblins in the mine, a beetle and a goblin of them on its upper level, and the troll and two goblins on its bridge; a placement may leash a patrol to its home. Slot 0 summons each kind. The difficulty scales their health and damage, and nothing of the wizard's: ×0.75 on Easy, ×1.5 on Hard.

| Enemy | How it fights | What hurts it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire wisp | Flies within 160 px of home and burns by touch; lights the scene and hums; outside a chase it drifts to burning balls, which heal it | Fire not at all; cold freezes it, and frozen it falls |
| Frost crawler | Walks its ledge, chills by touch for 15 and, after a hiss, leaps at a wizard within 300 px, about 220 px through the air and 290 px with its skid, but never into the staff light's glow: at its edge it shrinks back, plates raised, with a hiss and a puff of frost breath, so it lunges only at a wizard whose light is out | Fire twice over, cold not at all; it bleeds |
| Rust beetle | Paws the ground, a glint at its horn and dust at its foot, then charges with streaks and dust behind it, hurting whatever it meets, enemies too | A quarter of physical on its front, which turns bullets as iron does, and all of it from behind; half of fire, twice shock, and shock jumps to it; frozen, a light blow shatters it, and fast iron crushes it |
| Cave bat | Hangs at its roost, flush under a ceiling, from the start; flashes white, dives at the wizard and climbs back | It burns and bleeds; frozen, it falls and shatters when it lands |
| Goblin sapper | Skitters on its patrol, pausing to look back or skipping in place, hopping crates, rocks and low steps; cackles when it spots the wizard and keeps its distance, backing off when the wizard comes close and closing in from afar; between, it winds up and lobs a rock on an arc, or one throw in five a lit bomb that rolls and blows after its fuse; a wizard too close gets a quick stab of its knife, and it leaps back; badly hurt, it runs | It bleeds and burns; cold freezes it; a bomb snuffed by cold or a hole fizzles out |
| Cave troll | Walks at 91 px/s; grunts and slams the ground after a 0.8 s wind-up, at most once every 2 s, a ring of dust spreading from its fist, hurting everything near, kicking each body by its weight, shattering frozen things and shaking the view; swipes what is close in front of it, quicker; lifts an iron block, a bowling ball, a crate, or a frozen enemy or dummy and throws it at the wizard | It regenerates unless it burns; a blast's push and the wizard's blows never move it, the force push's wave only a little, and the black hole only slows it; its chill slows it more as it builds, it freezes at its threshold for 3 s, and it stuns only at three times it; its health shows over its head |

A killed enemy may drop an orb, the troll up to three: a health orb heals 20 and a mana orb gives back 25, drawn in from 192 px; a dropped orb pops out where its enemy fell, clear of the rock, falls to the ground below at 120 px/s unless drawn in, and blinks over its last 3 s before it fades at 20 s. Eight health orbs float around the level from the start, at least one in each area and two off the main way, and never fade; one taken stays gone through a save. A combat layer joins the music while an enemy sees the wizard.

## Saves

`src/save.rs` holds the save (schema 16): the run's next save ID, the random stream, the last area, the selected spell, the lantern and whether the staff light guttered, the summon's type, the cascades waiting, the throws in flight, and every saved entity by its save ID: the player's body, controller, health, stats, effects with their sources, abilities, ailment buckets, tags, the gun's reload, the tomes held and the pact's shield; the moving platforms and fire, and whether cold has put a fire out; each lava basin's chill and crust and the elevator's trip; the balls with their burn and ice, iron and scraps; the crates with their fire, the kegs and the goblins' bombs with their fuses, and the goblins' rocks; the open black holes; the dummies, the map's gone and the summoned; the enemies with their movers, machines, what they know and see, their health and effects, their attacks' targets and touches, and what a troll holds; the orbs; the missiles and bullets in flight. Loading spawns the map, takes off it the dummies and enemies that were gone and the books the player holds, writes each saved state over the map object with its save ID, spawns the rest from their prefabs, and names each effect's source, each attack's targets and touches and each troll's hold again. A save from the old map, schemas 1 to 6, is refused, its slot reading "Saved by an older version": every place and every map object's save ID moved with the new level. A schema 7 to 15 save migrates through each schema since: 8 adds the staff light to the player's abilities, 9 moves the save IDs of the thirteen map objects 0.99 added, 10 gives save IDs only to what a save names (the basins, the decks, the dummy, the enemies and the placed orbs), so no art change moves one, leaving the elevator at rest and setting a player saved in or under a lake on its nearest bank, 11 moves the one black hole into the list and keeps the gun's magazine to nine rounds, 12 moves the save IDs from the first placed orb's up past the map's seven goblins, which stand at their homes, its run without the map's new crates and kegs, 13 moves the player's saved point, his sprite's corner, onto the wizard's 96 px frame, so his body stands where it stood, 14 drops the hints, sets the player's maximum health to 100 with his health keeping its share, moves his pickup reach, a crawler's damage and a troll's walk to this version's and halves a cold build-up with the cold ladder's gates, and 15 sets each body saved in the start's new wall or left of the map at rest on the clearing's floor, at the first spot from the wall's face that meets no other body and skips the moving deck, the player first and at the spawn when none is clear, any other body with no room dropped and logged, a map dummy or enemy among them gone from the map, and 16 sets each body saved inside one of the tomes' three new supports at rest on its top, its x kept on it, with the same rule for one with no room. Presentation is not saved: particles, lights, glows, animation, flashes, shake, toasts, the HUD and the music. Each area's entry asks for an autosave (`shell::request_autosave`), which the shell writes to one of its three autosaves, an empty one, else one an older version wrote, else the oldest, else a damaged one, while its Autosave option is on.
