## Recorded limits

- **Two pushed-body cases the arrival pass leaves** (`D-092`), from the probe behind it. A pusher of 1,000 times the body's mass at 120–480 px/s can still crush the body through a 4 px dynamic gate (6 of 320 slow-push cases; 41 before the pass). At 15,360 px/s a pusher spawned after the body can end past the body it pushed (17 of 512). Nothing passes a static gate in either. Closing the first took a wall-last contact order plus static softness for heavy partners in the probe, which changes every pile.
- **A soft contact carries a bounded load, so a deep pile is compressed** (`D-063`, `D-094`). A contact under a steady load `L` rests at `linear_slop + L / (m_eff · ω²)` with `ω = 2π · min(contact_hertz, 0.25 / sub_dt)`, and its push stops growing once `bias_rate · (penetration − linear_slop)` reaches `max_push_speed`. At the defaults and gravity 3,600 (example 01) a ball-ball contact sags 0.20 px per ball weight and answers at most about 65 ball weights; a floor contact about 271. An 11,502-ball pile in the platformer's pit puts about 56 ball weights on each bottom small ball: at a steady 60 FPS the bottom 128 px sits at 0.57 of its non-overlapping spacing and the pile stands 733 px tall where about 950 px would be free of overlap. It is stable, costs a 13 ms step and sleeps; this is the load limit, not the slow-frame collapse `D-094` closed. The engine's own piles use gravity 900 and see a quarter of the sag. `contact_hertz` is the lever: 60 Hz reads stacking 1.06 against 1.32 and 951 px, while `max_push_speed` 480, 8 substeps and mass by area change nothing. It needed a constant substep first: 60 Hz softens from a 1/60 s frame, so with the step bound alone the pile's height followed the frame time, and at a frame time of 17–21 ms the pile never settled or slept. Since 0.55 `PhysicsPlugin` runs `physics_step` at the constant fixed step (`D-137`), so the lever waits as a [1.x backlog](plans/1.0/backlog-1.x.md) row: it moves the physics hash and every engine pile.
- **Kinematic bodies, sensors and queries** (`D-154`, M49's crowd probe). A sensor tests overlaps at the step's final positions, so one that moves farther in a step than its size plus a target's passes the target unreported; `cast_shape` along its path covers that case, and there is no exit event. Queries see the last step: a body moved since is found where the step left it, one spawned since is not found, a despawned one is skipped, and before the first step they find nothing. A `Position` write moves a kinematic body or a sensor as a teleport that no contact sees and that wakes nothing; moving by `Velocity` is what pushes and wakes. At 1/60 s a crowd of 500 circles pressing on a kinematic body reaches 5.81 px into it (circle body, spawned last), 5.76 (box, spawned last) and 5.01 (box, shuffled), over half a crowd radius; at 1/30 s and above, where such a world runs eight substeps, 1.62–2.74 px. A body pinned between a kinematic body and a wall ends 19.97–21.53 px inside the kinematic body, which nothing stops, and its centre crosses a static box's face though not a tile wall's, so W13c's mover casts before it moves. The lever not taken: moving a kinematic mover's pressed chain with it in `D-092`'s backward sweep.
- **The kit** (`D-155`, `D-172`, `D-173`, `D-175`, M50, M62, M63, M65). The transform hierarchy propagates positions only: a child keeps its own rotation and scale, and turning a parent does not swing its children around it. Children of a parent despawned at a command flush, by a plain `despawn` or the state stack's `SceneEntity` cleanup, draw one frame after it, until the next frame's first propagation pass takes them; `despawn_recursive`, or the parent's `SceneEntity` on each child, takes them in the same flush. `SoundOnEvent` plays one sound per event at the manifest volume, with no voice limit or bus, until W6c. A spawner that is a child of a moving parent carries a `Transform` only, written by the last frame's propagation, so its spawns land one frame behind the parent (M62). Commands flush once a frame, after `post_update`: a despawn queued in a frame's first fixed step is still alive in its second, and a spawn queued there is absent until the next frame, so the kit's recipes and its determinism hash are one-step-per-frame claims; a flush per step is the Phase 6 QA pass's question, since it changes the App's frame loop (M62, M63). A cue's `label` names a manifest font face that the load does not check: core's `FontRegistry` exists only after a window loads the fonts and lacks the engine font, and the harness loads none, so a misspelt face draws in the fallback chain's head with a warning; a core list of the manifest's faces would let the load check it (M63). A child that `SpawnChildren` queues draws one frame at its queue point before its first propagation, as a spawn lands at the flush after `post_update` (M63). A `prefab` cue on a subject that a game's system or the state stack despawned earlier in the frame's commands spawns nothing, since the spawn comes from the subject (`D-170`); the kit's own despawns, a `despawn` cue's target among them, queue after the step's spawns and mark the entity's descendants (M63, M65). A cue's labels outlive a state's exit by at most their seconds, since they are the kit's, not entities with a `SceneEntity` (M63). A cue reacts in the fixed step that reads its event, so an event sent outside the fixed step, as by `damage` from `update`, reacts one step later (M63).
- **Spawned entities and states** (`D-157`, `D-170`, M51, M60). A prefab queued from a source (`CommandBuffer::spawn_prefab_from`), as the kit's `Spawner` spawns, takes its source's `SceneEntity` under `StatePlugin`, and a source despawned earlier in the frame's commands spawns nothing. A prefab spawned with no source, from a system or a Tiled map, still takes none unless it passes one through `PreparedPrefab::with`. An entity that takes a state's marker in its exit frame after the dispatcher's listing, ahead of its despawns, outlives the state: one queued from a state's entity by a `pre_update` system ordered before `state_dispatcher`, or one spawned at once in `on_exit` with the marker through `with`. No kit system does either; a fix belongs to the state stack, since a sweep at the flush by state ID would also take the entities of a state that replaces itself.
- **The kit's controllers and helpers** (`D-158`, M52). The platformer controller and the top-down mover cast against the last step's positions: two controllers do not see each other's motion within a step, a body or tile spawned since the last step is unseen until the next, and nothing pushes a kinematic body out of an overlap, though a move may leave one, so a controller spawned inside a solid stays embedded. A controller's first step moves nothing. The platformer's carry on a kinematic ground is added uncast, so a platform can carry it into a wall. A lit sprite draws without its material, so it does not flash; a flash overrides a game `Tween` on the same override block, and a change made to a flashing sprite's material or block is lost at the restore. A trigger zone reports entering only, as `SensorEntered` does. A `TopDownMover` without a body moves its `Position` with no cast, so no wall stops it, and a zone or a trigger zone sees bodies only, so a bodiless entity never enters one, though a hitbox, contact damage or a pickup finds it on the kit's grid (M62, M65, `D-172`, `D-175`).
- **The kit's motion and facing** (`D-174`, M64). A bodiless mover (`linear_motion`, `steering`, `follow`, `orbit`, an oscillator, a knockback) moves its `Position` with no cast, so no wall stops it; a kinematic driver moves on rails, and only the controllers and a knockback cast. Nothing flips a sprite in place: a negative scale spans the quad leftward from its drawn point and `Sprite` has no flip field before W4b's sweep, so `Facing` turns and reports and `CharacterAnimator`'s clip sets show mirrored art. A platformer controller on an orbiting kinematic platform reads its velocity a step late, since `orbit` runs after the controllers to stay on a moving centre. A placer takes its target where the target's movers leave it, so a knockback, a speed cap, a wrap or a clamp that corrects the target later in the step reaches it a step late, and each placer in a chain of placers adds a step. The kit finds a kinematic body's driver by its components, so a body a game's own system drives takes no oscillator or knockback beside it. A dynamic body enters and leaves every step under its `speed_limit`, but gravity and contacts act inside the step, so its move is bounded only in free fall, at `(cap + g·dt)·dt` a step. `arrive`'s last approach may stop harder than its acceleration, an emergency stop, and with a radius larger than a step's travel it slows in proportion to the distance and settles rather than lands exactly. One kit writer per presentation channel: an oscillator's or a `Facing`'s rotation, an oscillator's scale, hides a tween's or a squash's on the same entity. A `nearest` target scans its filter's matches for each seeker, linear in seekers times matches, so turrets among a crowd wait for a nearest search on the kit's grid, which M65 left open, or a spatial query. `wrap` and `stay_in_view` judge the camera's view without its shake while `despawn_outside` judges the shaken view, until the two are aligned. A child's aim and facing read its drawn point, which the hierarchy writes after the sync, so a child aims a frame behind its parent.
- **The kit's overlap and damage** (`D-175`, M65). A hit shape on a child stands at the child's `Transform.position`, the last frame's propagation, with no sweep, so a child's hitbox can pass what its root's motion crossed inside a step; bodies stay on roots (`D-132`). Walls end projectiles only, and only tile layers and static colliders: a kinematic or dynamic body is never a wall, and a plain hitbox or a contact damage passes through walls, so a thrown blade that should stop composes `projectile`. A bounce leaves the projectile at the contact with its movers' velocities reflected and drops the rest of its step's travel. An ended projectile stands where it ended until the flush, but a homer's steering moves it from rest for the rest of its frame's steps. Zones and the `nearest` search stay off the grid: a zone sees bodies only and `nearest` scans its filter's matches. The grid's cells are 64 px, fixed.
- **The kit's damage types** (`D-176`, M66). A World holds 64 damage types; a load whose new types pass them fails, but a type past them that no load checked (an entry inserted in code, or one left without its file) gets no bit and one warning, so its parts carry no kind of it. A hit's source lends its `penetration` and `conversion` when its request resolves, so a source despawned before then lends nothing. `Struck` and the `hit` cue carry no kinds: a reaction to a hit's types before its resistances reads the hitting item's parts.
- **Data entries and the two-phase load** (`D-171`, M61). A commit checks every prefab and data entry loaded from a file, so two edits that need each other, such as a renamed tag and the prefab that names it, load only when saved in one hot-reload batch or followed by a manifest save; saved apart, each fails against the other's old file and the log says so. A manifest reload that fails keeps every previous entry and retries only at the next manifest save, since a file that never loaded is not routed. A reader that copied an entry, at a spawn say, keeps its copy across a reload; `DataRef::resolve` reads the entry now. A prefab or data reload does not check maps' objects again, as before M61. Layer 1 parses data files as JSON only, their kinds needing the game's plugins. Nearest-name suggestions cover prefab IDs, data IDs and kinds in prefab and data files only: scenes, component names, particle configs and tilemaps print none.
- **Screen transitions and mesh particles** (`D-093`). A transition's pass does not cover screen-space text, which draws after the post stack; a game fades its own text with `1 − StateStack::transition_cover()`, as example 03 does. Mesh particles draw above every sprite, so `z_order` cannot place them between sprites. At an odd window size the radial wipe leaves the center pixel at half brightness on the fully covered frame. Pixelate never hides the frame: the state changes under the coarsest mosaic. A live manifest edit of a particle mesh has not been exercised.
- **Text fallback ties and ellipsis limits** (`D-116`, `D-117`, M34). Faces load in sorted ID order, so a fresh start resolves fallback ties between faces of equal weight, stretch and style the same way every time, but a hot reload that adds or replaces a face appends it, and those ties may resolve differently until the next start; a canonical rebuild would restart fontdb IDs, which the glyph atlas and swash cache key glyphs by. An ellipsis line limit (`TextOverflow::Ellipsis { lines: Some(n) }`) counts lines per paragraph, since cosmic-text resets it at each newline; a block-wide limit waits for the first widget that needs one (W1 M2 or later).
- **Layer 1 loads each manifest alone** (`D-089`). `crates/tungsten-core/tests/manifests.rs` does not know the root sets, so a shipped material that names a shader of another root would fail there although the engine loads it. No shipped manifest crosses roots.
