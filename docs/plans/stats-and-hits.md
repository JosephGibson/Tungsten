## 2. Design

### 2.1 Lines and reads (PA-1, Step 1)

- **A line** is a source key, a stat and a `Modifier`, with optional filters: `with`, over the read's context; `while`, over the holder's tags; `against`, over the other party's tags at a hit (§4.1, §5.1). `Stats::add_line(stat, source, line)` beside `add_modifier`, which stays the unscoped line; `remove_modifiers(source)` takes both back. A stat without scoped lines keeps today's `Stat`; scoped lines ride one boxed extension on `Stats`. `add_line` keeps a line for a stat the entity lacks, for the reads that bring their own base (a sheet's, 2.6); `add_modifier` keeps today's rule (A1), and effects place their unscoped lines through it, so no existing effect stores anything new.
- **Data:** `stat_modifier` gains `with`, `while` and `against`; the `stats` scene form takes a number, as today, or `{ "base": 1, "lines": [ … ] }`, lines without `stat` under the source `base` (the entity's own data, §3.4). A save writes scoped lines beside `modifiers`; an older save loads unchanged.
- **Reads:** `Stats::get` is unchanged, the base and the unscoped lines (§5.1's empty context). `Stats::read(stat, &Read)` takes the context as several tag sets matched together without merging them, the holder's tags and the other party's; `Stats::sums(stat, &Read)` returns `(base + Σadd, Σpct, Πmul)` and the winning override, and which lines a read deferred, for sheets and the defender. `while` lines reach context reads only (Q1).
- **Stacking:** §5.3 in `f64`, in add order (Q3); unscoped overrides keep the last-added rule; a scoped override is refused at load and by `add_line` (T9).
- **Well-known stats:** `crit_chance` in `[0, 1]`, `crit_multiplier` in `[1, ∞)` and `damage_taken` in `[0, ∞)` join `WELL_KNOWN` (7 → 10), so every genre's source can crit and every target can take damage by context without a declaration (PA-Q3).

### 2.2 World sources (PA-10, Step 1)

- **`WorldSource`**: a `TagFilter` and lines. **`WorldSources`**, a resource: sources by name, a revision bumped when a source changes. It is not a saved resource: a registered saved name writes its version into every capture, present or not and 02's `saves` row builds `StatsPlugin` and counts its capture's bytes (A25); a game sets its sources at a run's start, a load's included, as 01's `enemies::start` does from `on_enter` (A18). `set(world, name, source)` replaces one (equal lines bump nothing), `remove(world, name)` takes it back, `apply_to(world, entity)` brings one entity's lines up to date at once, for a spawn a game's code reads before the next tick.
- **Applied:** every entity with `Stats` keeps the revision it was brought to (neither saved nor compared). `stats_apply`'s loop notes a stale one; only on a frame that met one does it run its world-source query (`Entity`, `&mut Stats`, `Option<&Tags>`): each source's lines leave, under the source's name as their key, and those whose filter passes the entity's tags come back; then the loop runs again, so a new spawn's consumers take the source in the tick its stats first apply. A world with no source never meets a stale entity, so it runs today's one query (A22).
- **Live:** a source set or removed reaches the living at the next tick, or at once through `apply_to`; a filter is read then, so a tag change after spawn needs `apply_to`. Names the resource has held stay known, so a removed source's lines leave too. An entity whose lines under a source already equal the source's keeps them where they are, so a load (every revision reads 0) followed by the run's `set`, or a repeated `set`, reorders no modifier and changes no byte of a later save. The resource is inserted by the first `set`.

### 2.3 A hit's context, side and the defender (PA-7, P7-7, Step 2)

- **The request** gains context tags (`with_context`, a skill's or a game's, such as 01's `hit.blast`), state tags (`with_state`) and a direction (`with_direction`, the way the hit travels), behind one boxed extension that only a request carrying one of them allocates, so its size holds (A22). Its side rides inline, one byte beside its flags: `strike_with` sets it at the strike from the blow's direction (away from the hitter, else along the relative motion, A8) against the target's `Aim`, so a kit hit allocates nothing it did not; a game's `with_direction` is judged against the target's `Aim` at the resolve. The extension saves with a queued request.
- **Kit tags,** declared by the kit so data names them and the genericity check holds (no damage type): the tracks `delivery.hit` and `delivery.dot` (a periodic request's), the states `delivery.pierced`, `delivery.forked`, `delivery.chained` and `delivery.repeated` (8.2 sets them; a test may), and the sides `side.front` and `side.rear`.
- **The side:** a target with an `Aim` takes `side.front` when the direction points against its aim (`direction · aim < 0`), else `side.rear`; no direction or no `Aim`, no side (Q9).
- **The defender's read,** per landed part, in §6.4's order: the read's context is the request's context, its track, its states and side, and the part's type with, for a sheet's part, its history (§6.4 step 5); the attacker's tags are the snapshot's holder tags for a request built from a delivery (2.6), else its source's `Tags` at the hit. A factor of 0 stays immunity; each matching factor (its unscoped modifiers as today, plus scoped resist lines whose filters pass) rises to its tag's floor; a factor below 1 gains the part's penetration, capped at 1, except on a periodic request (§6.5); the factors multiply; then the target's `damage_taken`, read the same way, multiplies the part.
- **Floors:** `Resist::with_floor(tag, floor)`; the `resist` scene form takes a factor or `{ "factor": 0.5, "floor": 0.25 }`; `resist_modifier` gains `with`, `against` and `floor` (a modifier on the floor).
- **Unchanged path:** a request without the extension on a target with no scoped resist line, no floor and no `damage_taken` resolves as A6 does, allocation-free.

### 2.4 Crit (PA-5, Step 3)

- **One rule,** decided once per request: by the game (`with_crit(b)`: carried, no draw, no factor, as today); by its builder (`DamageRequest::roll_crit(world)`: from the source's entity-level `crit_chance` and `crit_multiplier`, one draw when the chance is above 0, every part times the multiplier on a crit, marked); or, when neither did, at the resolve: a non-periodic request whose source has a chance above 0 draws once, first, before the take-hits gate, in the pipeline's sorted order, and a crit multiplies every part before conversion and the defences (Q10). A missing multiplier is 1.5. A periodic request never draws (T11). A sheet's request takes its own sums (2.6).
- **Draws:** only where a source has a chance, so a game without one draws what it drew before (§6.6); no recipe fixture, 01 entity but the player, or template entity holds one (A3).

### 2.5 01 on them (Step 4)

- **Crit:** the player prefab's `stats` hold `crit_chance` 0.05 and `crit_multiplier` 2; `Hit::request` drops its roll and calls `roll_crit` after building its parts, so A17's test still reads the crit at build, with one draw. `CRIT_CHANCE` and `CRIT_FACTOR` stay as the prefab's values' names, which 01's tests read, and `player::spawn` debug-asserts the prefab matches them. The player is the only 01 entity with a chance, and its hits are all built by `Hit` (A20), so every draw stays where it was.
- **Blast share:** `Hit::blast()` puts `hit.blast` in the request's context instead of halving it; the player's `damage_taken` is `{ "base": 1, "lines": [{ "modifier": { "mul": 0.5 }, "with": { "all": ["hit.blast"] } }] }`; 01's tags declare `hit.blast`; `PLAYER_BLAST_SHARE` names the line's value under the same assertion. A factor of 0.5 is exact in `f64` and `f32`, so the half taken after the resistances lands every amount the half before did.
- **Difficulty:** `enemies::scale(world, enemy, difficulty)` sets the world source `difficulty` (filter `team.enemy`, `mul factor(difficulty)` on `max_health` and `damage`) and calls `apply_to(world, enemy)`, so every spawn path, the summon a step later and the 14 test calls keep their values; `factor()` stays the numbers' one copy (Q5). A saved enemy's `difficulty` lines re-apply under the same key, to the same values.
- **Saves:** schema 24; `to_24` gives an older save's player the three stats when its `Stats` lack them, so a migrated current capture is unchanged.
- **Files:** each data change in its `tools/content/` source and its `assets/` copy, byte for byte, under `just level-check` (A20).

### 2.6 Sheets and deliveries (PA-2, Step 5)

- **Data (§4.2):** an `abilities` entry gains `tags`, its context C (`skill.*` by convention), and `skill`: `bases` (`damage`, a damage type to `[min, max]`; `crit_chance`; `cost`; `cooldown`), `levels` (a base to a `curves` ID read at the skill's level, a range scaled about its midpoint) and `lines` (skill-local, under `skill:<entry>`). An owner's entry gains `level`, 1–40, default 1. `Abilities::add_skill_lines(entry, source, lines)` and `remove_skill_lines(entry, source)` place a skill-local source (`support:<id>` at 8.7). The line kinds beside stat lines are one-of shapes with their filters inside each form (A23's description test probes a one-of field alone): `added` (`{ type: [min, max] }`) and `pen` (`{ type: points }`) here, `convert` and `extra` in Step 6.

A line with `against`, or a state tag in `with`, is deferred to the hit; only `damage`, `crit_chance`, `crit_multiplier` and `pen` lines may be (§5.4), so one on `skill_level`, `cost`, `cooldown` or an `added` line is refused at load and by `add_line` and `add_skill_lines`, while a defender's `damage_taken` and resist lines read them at the hit (2.3) and a game's own stats keep them for its own reads. `while` reads the holder's tags at the use. The sheet's cost and cooldown replace the ability's. An ability without `skill` uses as today.
- **The snapshot, `Delivery`** (§3.5): per part its range, type, history, both tracks' sums and penetration; the crit sums with their overrides; the context and the holder's tags, the attacker's tags for every `against` the hit reads, a defender's included; the deferred lines; the state tags; the holder for credit while it exists. It is saved (`delivery`, registered by `AbilitiesPlugin`, the holder by save ID), and it never reads its user again (T1). A hit after its holder is gone has no source, before a save and after a load alike, since a save keeps a reference only to an entity with a save ID and a load resolves only live ones (A26); its damage is the snapshot's either way.
- **At the resolve** (§6.4 steps 1–3): the deferred lines fold in, `against` on the target's tags and `with` on the state tags; one range draw shared by every part, each at the same `u` (T7); then one crit draw under the chance, clamped to 1, so a sheet's request draws twice; the multiplier; then 2.3's defences with each part's own penetration and no conversion. `parts()` on such a request reads each part's range midpoint before the draw.

### 2.7 Conversion and extra (PA-6, Step 6)
