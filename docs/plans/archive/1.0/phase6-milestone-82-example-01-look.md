### 2.1 The wizard's art (step 1)

- **One rig, a 96 px frame.** `tools/actors.py` takes the sheet's `wizard.py` in place of the courier's rig: the courier's skeleton and poses moved into a 96 × 96 frame (the body unchanged, half (20, 28), its centre at (48, 65)), with its own `WIZARD = 96` and `WIZARD_SOLE = 91`, so the dummy, the goblin and the props keep `SIZE` 64 and `SOLE` 59. A long robe with bell sleeves, a mantle, a rope belt and pouch, brass trim and sewn stars; a big felt hat that bends back and flops, with a patch, a band and buckle and a crescent; a bulbous nose under a bushy brow and a white beard that swings with the move; a gnarled staff with a leather grip and a sprig, its crook holding the orb. Indigo by default (Q1).
- **The orb is the light.** Plain amber glass (`J` `K` `L` and a `Z` glint), never emissive: dull while the light is off, shining under the glow's hot core while it burns (A16; the sheet's §2 shows both). No other courier pixel uses `y` or `Y` now, and the lantern, `cast_frame` and `check_frame` go. A frame's anchor is the orb's centre, which the rig returns with the frame: `build_player` hands the anchors to `placement.prepare` in place of the scan (A5), mirrored about x 48 for `_left`.
- **Clips.** `ex10_player_{idle,walk,jump,fall,land,double_jump,toss}` keep their names and frame counts, `ex10_player_death` its name; `ex10_player_aim` keeps five frames, now the gun's pose: the pistol in the far hand along the aim, the staff upright. The death (the owner's two notes) falls back onto the ground in seven frames, 100 110 110 90 80 120 300 ms (910 ms, so he lies still for about a second of `DEATH_SECS`' 2, `src/features/run/mod.rs:47`): struck, he reels back on one foot as the hat lifts off and the staff is flung ahead; he sits down hard; topples back; lands flat on his back with a puff of dust; rebounds a pixel; and rests face up, a hand on his belly, his toes up, the hat landed upright behind his head, the staff along the ground with its orb past his feet. The first four frames pose the upright rig; the last three turn it a quarter anticlockwise in whole pixels (`wizard.py`'s `death_frames` and `supine`); a hatless head draws a bald crown in a grey fringe (`WPose.bare`). New: `ex10_player_hurt` (2 frames, 90 110 ms: knocked back, the staff jolted), `ex10_player_bolt` (80 120 100: the staff drawn back over the shoulder, then thrust at the aim, the orb flared), `ex10_player_push` (80 120 100: the free hand shoves out palm first, the staff raised behind), `ex10_player_conjure` (100 90 110: the staff raised high, then struck down), `ex10_player_staff` (5 frames, the sprays' pose: the staff levelled along the aim in both hands).
