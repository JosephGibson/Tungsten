## 2. Design

Level B: 01's code and data only, no public API. The art-review sheet (§2.7) is the reference for every look below; its scripts are a prototype the run ports into the tree's generator and code, never imports.

### 2.1 The floating check

`placement.floating(source, tiled, images)` returns every finding as a line `kind name x y detail`, sorted by x; it reads the map `build_level` made, after `prepare`.

- **Solid** is the drawn terrain layer: a pixel is solid where its cell's terrain sprite is opaque (alpha ≥ 128). Slabs, decks and iron are drawn in that layer, so they count. **Liquid** is the river, pool, lava and channel-water props' opaque pixels.
- **Classes**, by the prop's `anchor`, else by its sprite's list in `scenery.py`:
  - `ground` (and a hand-placed `LEVEL_PROPS` sprite): at least `MOST` (0.6) of the pixel columns of its bottom two opaque rows have solid within `GAP` (2) px under them, and no column's foot is more than `SINK` (6) px inside solid;
  - `under` (and a hand-placed `HUNG_PROPS` sprite): at least `MOST` of the columns of its top two opaque rows have solid from `GAP` px above to `UNDER_TUCK` px into them;
  - `wall`: an opaque pixel lies within 3 px of solid to its side (the roots, the gargoyle on the channel's face);
  - `back`: every opaque pixel lies over a background-layer tile (the hollow's fissure);
  - streams, by sprite (`pour`, `falls`, `lavafall` and the new `pour_wide`, `pour_foot`): each stack's top meets its source within `GAP` px (solid, or a spill, a spout, the gargoyle or the fissure drawn there), each segment's top meets the drawn foot of the one above within `GAP` px, its last segment's foot meets the first solid or liquid under it within `GAP` px, and no segment is more than a quarter inside solid;
  - exempt by name: haze, plumes, mists, the far pieces, the water, river, pool and lava props and the spills, which sit in or on what holds them;
  - any other prop fails as `unjudged`, so a new piece states how it stands.
- **Groups**: each group's composite, from its own pieces where they landed, as its anchor says. A piece inside two groups' boxes, or a group with none, fails.
- **Supports**: each support's last `timber_support` piece stands.
- **Decorations** (A34), by sprite: each pier column, behind the aqueduct or in front at the bridge, ends on a `pier_base` over terrain or a `pier_cutwater` over the river; each arcade springing, back, front or in a gap's strip, has a capital or pier top beside it; a parapet's bottom row meets the deck or terrain in the cell under it; a background structure over terrain (the cave mouth) fills its bottom row to its tiles' foot, so no backdrop shows between it and the rock drawn over it; the far mists are exempt; any other decoration sprite fails as `unjudged`.
- **Output**: `generate.py --floating` prints the lines and a total and exits 1 on any (step 1). From step 2 `build_level` raises `ValueError` naming every line, so `--check`, the write and the tests fail on any.

### 2.2 Standing on the drawn surface

`prepare(source, images, tiles)` takes the terrain tiles, which `build_level` makes first: `terrain_cells` and `terrain_tiles` read only the platforms, so they move ahead of `prepare` with the random stream's order unchanged (the run confirms they read nothing `prepare` writes). On every support, the terrain families, slabs and decks alike, since each is drawn in the terrain layer, `prepare` places each supported prop and group by the check's own test (§2.1): the smallest move from today's place, 0 to 12 px down for a grounded one or up for a hung one, at which the stand or hang rule passes on the same columns with the same `GAP`, `SINK` and `MOST`. On wet and mine rock that is 1–4 px down; under basalt 2–5 px up; on ground, basalt tops, slabs and decks' straight tops nothing; under a weathered deck's uneven planks it fits the mine's hanging lantern and chain.

The nine left (A3, A9) are fixed by hand, today's sprites kept: the pour at 112.8125 ends at row 37, its foot on `aq_rubble_1`'s top; the gargoyle's pour starts at 29.5 under its jaws and those at 144.156 and 148.8125 at 29 under their breaks; the gargoyle takes `anchor: wall` on `channel_east_a`; the fissure `anchor: back`; the lavafall is restacked from the fissure's drawn foot (about 17 px over today's first segment) down to the lava's skin, its segments contiguous and the last overlapping the one before, as the gargoyle's pour is; `bones_1` moves out of `hollow_step`'s face onto `hollow_floor_2` (about 248.3); `boulder_2` moves to about 313.6, clear of `cut_floor_2`'s end. The ravine's vine groups each take their own ledge's pieces, and `prepare` refuses a piece inside two groups' boxes (A6).

### 2.3 The start

As the sheet's `proto_start.py` lays it out:

- **The wall:** a `ground` platform `start_wall`, columns 0–1 from row 21 to the map's foot (its cells under row 36 the clearing's): a 15-row rock face under the tests' sky, drawn by today's rules (its right side `cliff_right`, a mossy top), as `east_wall_end` is at the map's east end. - **The boundary:** `level::BOUNDARY`, a static box from x −64 to 0 and from `WORLD_MIN.y` to `WORLD_MAX.y`, in the slabs' stone layer (`SLAB_LAYER`) meeting every layer, drawing nothing, spawned by `level::spawn` with the gameplay state, saved by no one. - **The props:** the crates at 12.2, 13.10625 and 12.653125 (tier 1), the keg at 14.1 and the dummy at x 15.0, right of the moving deck (A16) and under the perch; the oak group and its roots two columns right, the vines at 3, the lantern at 5.5 with its embers, the marker at 4.6. - **Saves (schema 15):** `to_15` sets each saved body whose box overlaps the wall's (x 0–128, rows 21–35) or the boundary's (x < 0) on the clearing's floor, at the first x from 128 to the terrace's face at 1,024 where its box overlaps no other saved body's, skipping the moving deck's columns (556.8–723.2, A16), its velocity zeroed. The player goes first; with no clear spot it goes to the run's spawn point, where the solver separates any dynamic body from it, so a save never fails to load. Any other body with no room is dropped: a spawned one leaves its list, and a map dummy or enemy also joins `gone`, so the load removes the map's instance rather than restoring it fresh (`src/save.rs:1303`, `:1382`); each dropped save ID goes in the migration's log line. Bodies are the player, dummies, enemies, balls, blocks, scrap, props and orbs, each by its saved point and its kind's offset and half extents (A22), frozen in `save.rs` beside the migration as earlier schemas' constants are.
