### 2.2 Shared pieces

- `shared::body_centre(world, entity) -> Option<Vec2>` and `shared::surface_distance(at, centre, shape) -> f32` replace A1's copies; `player::centre` stays the name the features call, over it.
- `toys::radial_push(world, at, radius, speed)`: each dynamic body whose centre is within `radius`, by `speed × (1 − d / radius)` over its mass, as `push_bodies` does today; each live enemy whose body centre is within `radius` through `knock_back` at `speed × (1 − d / radius) × its kind's pull`, the hole's own response, so both gravity spells move each kind alike, and `knock_back` applies the knockback's resist. Direction away from `at`, up at the centre. Measured by centre throughout. The hole's pull stays its own (A3): a knockback each step would lock the walkers' input for as long as they stay in reach.
- A kind that resists knockback whole takes no push from 01: `KindInfo::resist` restates each prefab's knockback resist (A33; troll 1, beetle 0.5, the rest 0), held to the prefabs by `tests/game/data.rs`; `radial_push` skips such a kind, and `combat::Hit::request` leaves out the push of a hit on one, so neither a push nor a gun's, a body's or iron's pushed hit launches the troll at zero and locks its input (A4). The kit's own hitboxes and touches still do, a known issue (step 9).
- `shared::spawn_key_burst(world, name, at)`: at the cap, the oldest cosmetic burst gives up its slot, drained at once and no longer counted, its particles left to fade, and `burst_cleanup` sweeps it as it does a burst. Deaths, gore, summons, shatters and refusals spawn key bursts; the rest stay cosmetic. `Burst` gains its spawn order and whether it is key; presentation, never saved.

### 2.3 Black holes

At most `HOLE_CAP` 3 open, each for 6 s; a fourth collapses the lowest save ID first. Over its last 0.5 s a hole shrinks to nothing, and when it closes, by time or by the cap, it plays `HoleCollapsed` with a small gravity ring (`Ring::Collapse`, no push). Its layers draw in 01's sprite base between the tiles and the `Sprite` entities, so actors, balls, iron, particles and props draw over it (Q3), at `z_norm` 1.0, the tiles' far plane, not `push_quad`'s 0, so the order holds under `gpu_depth` too though 01 runs `cpu_stable` (A36); the rings and sprays stay after the sprites. One shared hum voice, `ex10_black_hole_hum_sfx`, starts silent with the run's music, its volume by the nearest open hole's distance to the courier, silent past 900 px and ducked as the wisps' hum is (Q2). The broom: in the overlap phase, small balls whose centre lies within 24 px of an open hole's centre go, nearest first and then by save ID, at most 12 a hole a step, every eighth with `ex10_poof`. Each hole's particles are cut to a third (300 sparks, 100 dust), so three holes take 1,200 of the 2,048.

### 2.4 Iron

Mass 400, scraps 25, so a block's sixteen scraps still sum to it. `iron_weight`, in the move phase before the physics step, adds 1,800 px/s² (half the world's 3,600) to a block's velocity while it falls faster than 30 px/s, never while `Held` or in `Throws` (A13). A body hit from iron (a block or a scrap, thrown or not) carries a push along the closing line of 0.6 × the closing speed, at most 900 px/s, through the hit's push, which the kit gives a hurt target and never a killed one (A33), and none to the troll (§2.2).
