## 2. Design

### 2.1 The motion write rule

Decision 174's core, w13-kit §2 rule 6 made exact:

1. **Movers move roots before the step.** A mover writes a bodiless or static entity's `Position`, and its `Velocity` when it has one so that `Facing` and the animator read its speed, or a kinematic or dynamic body's `Velocity`. It never writes the `Transform.position` of an entity with a `Position` (the sync's, A5) or of a child (the hierarchy's, A14); an entity with a `Parent` is skipped with one warning (Q6). On a bodiless entity every mover moves `Position` by an increment, so movers add up in any order; the placers (`Follow`, `Orbit`) set a point instead. The placers and the path follower fold their entity's oscillator offset into their goal, since each brings its entity back onto its goal every step (`kit/motion/path.rs:399` for the path follower), which would undo the oscillator's increments.
2. **A kinematic body has one driver.** The step keeps a kinematic body's velocity (A35), so exactly one kit mover, its driver, writes it every step, zero while it holds: the path follower, a controller, `LinearMotion`, `Steering`, `Follow` or `Orbit`. The adders run after the driver and add to its write: `Oscillator`'s position after `linear_motion`, `Knockback` after every driver and the oscillator. With no driver, the oscillator drives, writing its wave's change over the step as the path follower writes its path, and a knockback adds to it; with neither, a knockback writes its slide while it pushes and zero on the step it ends. A driver that runs after the oscillator (a controller, `top_down_mover`, `steering`) keeps the body's velocity: the oscillator warns once and leaves that body. A dynamic body keeps its own velocity: the setters (`LinearMotion`, `Follow`, `Orbit`) skip it with one warning, `Steering` steers it and a knockback adds to it once. A game's own system that drives a kinematic body is no driver the kit can see (Q13).
3. **Constraints correct after the step.** `Wrap` and `StayInView` run in `kit_overlap` and write `Position` for every body kind, or `Transform.position` without one: a wrap is a teleport and writes `PrevPosition` with it, so the sync draws no streak; a clamp keeps `PrevPosition` and zeroes the outward velocity. `SpeedLimit` caps the kit's writes and every body's velocity last in `kit_move`, and a dynamic body's velocity again after the solver in `kit_overlap` (A34).
4. **Presentation writes once a frame in `kit_present`:** `Transform.rotation` (`Facing` or an oscillator), `Transform.scale` (an oscillator), `Sprite.z_order` (`DepthSort`), and `AnimationState` with, on the frame its clip changes, `Sprite.asset_id` (`CharacterAnimator`, after `animation_tick`): one kit writer per channel per entity. Tweens and squash write rotation and scale earlier in `post_update` (A5, A11), so on the same entity the kit's write wins and hides theirs; `Facing` never touches scale.
5. **Gameplay reads no presentation.** Fixed-step items read `Position`, `Velocity`, `Intent` and `Aim`, never `Facing` or a transform's rotation, so a recipe holds at any frame rate.
6. **Bodiless needs no walls.** A bodiless mover is stopped by nothing, as the top-down mover is today, and a kinematic driver moves on rails; only the controllers and `Knockback` cast.

A crate-private `motion/drive.rs` (step 2) holds the body kinds (child, bodiless, static, kinematic, dynamic, unplaced), whether a kinematic body has a driver, read from the drivers' components, and these writes, with one warning per entity and reason.

### 2.2 Shared: points and targets

- **Points.** An item measures an entity at its `Position`, else its `Transform.position`: the point the default extract draws as the sprite's top-left (A10). `Follow`, `Orbit` and `Aim` take an `offset` to work from a sprite's centre; nothing centres by itself (Q10).
- **`Target`**, a public value type in `motion/target.rs`, data with private fields:
  - `{ "source": {} }`: the entity's `Source` (A15), the spawn chain's origin.
  - `{ "nearest": { "all": ["team.player"], "any": [], "none": [], "range": 320 } }`: the nearest other entity whose `Tags` pass the filter, within `range` when given; ties go to the lower entity index. The load checks each tag (`target.nearest.all.0`, A31). It names an `all` or an `any` tag: an entity without `Tags` passes a filter of `none` alone (A37), so the load refuses one with neither, naming the field, and `Target::nearest` panics on it (Q14).
  - `{ "point": [640, 360] }`: a world point.
  - Rust: `Target::source()`, `Target::nearest(filter)` with `with_range(r)` (finite, above 0), `Target::point(v)` (finite). No entity form, since `Entity` has no serde (A15): a game that targets one entity tags it or sets its `Source`. Components skip the resolved entity in serde and resolve it again each step.
  - Described as the one-of shape `target` (three forms) and the shape `nearest`.
- **The nearest search** (step 2). Each system run gathers, once per distinct filter, the tagged entities that `TagFilter::matches` passes, their indices and points sorted by index; each seeker scans its filter's matches. A step costs one pass over the tagged entities plus seekers times matches (Q4).
- **A target's point when placing.** `Follow` and `Orbit` place their entity on where the target's movers leave it this step: a kinematic or dynamic body's `Position` plus its `Velocity` times the step, since the step integrates those two kinds; a static body's or a bodiless entity's `Position` as its movers leave it (by the edges of §2.4), since the step integrates neither (`crates/tungsten-core/src/physics/step/mod.rs:605-611`); else its `Transform.position`. A knockback, a speed cap, a wrap or a clamp that corrects the target later in the step reaches the placer on the next step, a recorded limit: the placers run before `knockback` and `speed_limit` so that a knocked-back or capped placer is pushed and capped itself, and the constraints come after the physics step.

### 2.3 Item cards

Common to all thirteen: no events in or out, no data kind, no trace kind (motion is continuous, so the cue vocabulary stays A's); one scene component under the bare name with a private DTO under `deny_unknown_fields`, numbers checked at deserialization; a component with private fields behind constructors and `with_*` builders that derives `Serialize` and `Deserialize` with its state; inspector rows under the type's name; rustdoc with a doctest; a unit test file; a harness test; recipes; a description; a bench row. Movers' scene components spawn `Position` and `PrevPosition` at the spawn point, so a prefab without a `rigid_body` moves bodiless, but no `Velocity`, which stays a body's.

**`LinearMotion`** · `linear_motion` · `LinearMotionPlugin` · `LINEAR_MOTION` in `Move`
- Data: `{ "velocity": [0, 120], "acceleration": [0, 0] }`; `velocity` required, `acceleration` `[0, 0]`, both finite.
- Moves by its velocity, which its acceleration changes, exact for a constant acceleration (`Δ = (v + a·dt/2)·dt`, then `v += a·dt`), so its path is the same at any step.
- Writes as a setter (§2.1). Edges: before both controllers, as `path_follow`, so a rider reads its platform's velocity.
- Recipes: platformer (a conveyor carries the hero), shmup (an asteroid drifts and wraps). Rows: `velocity`, `acceleration`. Bench: `linear_motion`, bodiless.

**`Oscillator`** · `oscillator` · `OscillatorPlugin` · `OSCILLATOR` in `Move`, `OSCILLATOR_PRESENT` in `Present`
- Data: `{ "position": { "amplitude": [0, 3], "period": 1.2 }, "rotation": { "amplitude": 0.3, "period": 0.8 }, "scale": { "amplitude": 0.1, "period": 0.5 }, "phase": 0 }`: at least one channel; each `period` above 0; `position.amplitude` a finite offset at the peak; `rotation.amplitude` finite radians; `scale.amplitude` in `[0, 1)`; `phase` in `[0, 1)` of a period, shared by the channels. Shapes `position_wave` and `wave`. Spawns `Position` and `PrevPosition` only with a position channel.
- A sine per channel on its own clock, advanced on the fixed step and kept at its last two steps. The position channel adds the wave's change each step, so it composes with the increments of other motion; beside a placer or a path follower it adds nothing, since they fold the wave's offset into their goals (`path_follow` gains that fold in step 2). Rotation and scale are written each frame from the base the entity had at its first frame, at the clock drawn as the sync draws positions, `prev + (cur − prev)·alpha` while `Time::interpolate` is on and `cur` otherwise (A5), so they hold while paused.
- Writes: position as an adder, or as the driver of a kinematic body that has none (§2.1); `Transform.rotation`; `Transform.scale`. Edges: after `linear_motion`, whose velocity it adds to; before both controllers, so a bobbing platform carries its rider.
- Recipes: platformer (a bobbing lift carries the hero), top-down (a gem bobs and pulses; a pet bobs as it follows); the template's hazard. Rows: channels, clock. Bench: `oscillator` (position, bodiless) and `oscillator_present` (rotation and scale).

**`Follow`** · `follow` · `FollowPlugin` · `FOLLOW` in `Move`
- Data: `{ "target": { "source": {} }, "offset": [0, -24], "lag": 0.15, "max_speed": 300 }`; `target` required; `offset` `[0, 0]`; `lag` at least 0, default 0; `max_speed` above 0, default `null` (none).
- Each step closes `1 − e^(−dt/lag)` of the way to its goal, all of it at lag 0, at most `max_speed·dt`: the target's point (§2.2) plus its offset, plus its own oscillator's offset when it has one, so a follower can bob about its follow point; holds while no target resolves.
- Writes as a setter; a kinematic body's `Velocity` reaches the goal at the step's end, zero while it holds. Edges: after `path_follow`, `linear_motion`, `oscillator`, both controllers and `steering`, the movers a target moves by.
- Recipes: platformer (a firefly trails the hero), top-down (a pet follows with lag). Rows: target, offset, lag; a cross at its goal. Bench: `follow`, one target.

**`Orbit`** · `orbit` · `OrbitPlugin` · `ORBIT` in `Move`
- Data: `{ "center": { "source": {} }, "radius": 48, "speed": 3, "angle": 0, "offset": [0, 0] }`; `center` a target, default `null`, its spawn point kept from its first step; `radius` at least 0, required; `speed` finite radians a second, required, positive turning clockwise on screen (y down); `angle` radians, default 0 (pointing right); `offset` `[0, 0]`.
- Places its point at `centre + offset + radius·(cos θ, sin θ)`, plus its own oscillator's offset when it has one, `θ = angle + speed·t` on its own fixed-step clock, the centre placed as §2.2 says; with its target gone it keeps circling the last centre.
- Writes as a setter. Edges: those of `Follow`, so an orbiter stays on a moving centre; none before the controllers (Q7).
- Recipes: top-down (blades a wielder's spawner spawns circle their source, A32), platformer (a firebar's ball circles its spawn point). Rows: centre, radius, angle; its circle drawn. Bench: `orbit`, around its spawn point.

**`Steering`** · `steering` · `SteeringPlugin` · `STEERING` in `Move`
- Data: `{ "target": { "nearest": { "all": ["team.player"] } }, "mode": "seek", "max_speed": 90, "acceleration": 600, "radius": 64 }`; `target` required; `mode` one of `seek` (default), `flee`, `arrive`; `max_speed` above 0, required; `acceleration` above 0, default `null` (the desired velocity at once); `radius` above 0, default 64: `arrive` slows inside it, `flee` flees inside it, `seek` ignores it.
- Steers its velocity toward the desired one by at most `acceleration·dt` a step, capped at `max_speed`: `seek` full speed at the target, `flee` full speed away inside the radius and braking outside, `arrive` at the least of `max_speed`, `max_speed` scaled by its distance over the radius and, with an acceleration, `√(2·acceleration·distance)`, so it brakes in time, and never further in a step than the distance left, so it lands on the target and stops however small the radius: on that last approach the stop may exceed `acceleration`, an emergency stop; with no target it brakes. Its velocity is the body's `Velocity` when it has one, so a dynamic body's contacts carry into its next step (A29), else its own.
- Writes: a bodiless entity's `Position` (and `Velocity` when present); a kinematic or dynamic body's `Velocity`; skips static bodies and children. Stats: `move_speed` as `max_speed`, written by `stats_apply` (a sixth column, A9). Edges: none.
- Recipes: top-down (a seeker chases the hero, a coward flees inside its radius), shmup (a mine arrives at the ship). Rows: mode, target, velocity. Bench: `steering`, seeking one target.

**`Knockback`** · `knockback` · `KnockbackPlugin` · `KNOCKBACK` in `Move`; `knock_back(world, entity, velocity) -> bool`
- Data: `{ "seconds": 0.2, "resist": 0 }`; `seconds` above 0, default 0.2; `resist` in `[0, 1]`, default 0.
- `knock_back` starts a push of `velocity × (1 − resist)`, replacing one that runs, and returns false, pushing nothing, for an entity without `Knockback`, a dead one or a velocity that is not finite. On a `PlatformerController` it launches the controller and locks its input for `seconds` (A8), whose gravity and walls carry it; on a dynamic body it adds the push to `Velocity` once; otherwise the system adds the push, falling linearly to 0 over `seconds`, to the entity's motion each step.
- Writes: an adder: a bodiless entity's `Position` (and `Velocity` when present); a kinematic body slides by a cast from where its driver's velocity ends (`move_and_slide`, A30), so the top-down mover's walls hold it, and the slide joins its `Velocity`, the oscillator's when that drives, or with neither is its `Velocity`, zero on the step the push ends (§2.1); static bodies and children take no push. Edges: after `path_follow`, `linear_motion`, `oscillator`, `top_down_mover`, `steering`, `follow` and `orbit`, whose velocity it adds to.
- Recipes: top-down (a bodiless rover pushed back; a kinematic brawler pushed into a wall slides along it), platformer (an acrobat launched back, its input locked). Rows: push, time left. Bench: `knockback`, bodiless with pushes running.

**`SpeedLimit`** · `speed_limit` · `SpeedLimitPlugin` · `SPEED_LIMIT` in `Move`, last; `SPEED_LIMIT_SETTLE` in `Overlap`
- Data: `{ "speed": 600 }`, `{ "x": 240 }`, `{ "y": 900 }` or any combination, at least one, each above 0.
- `speed_limit` caps, before the step, the length first, then each axis: every body's `Velocity`, the movers' own velocities (a platformer controller's, linear motion's, steering's), so their next step starts capped, and a bodiless entity's step move (`Position − PrevPosition`) with its `Velocity` when present; a kinematic body or a bodiless entity never moves past the cap, and a dynamic body enters every step at or under it, whatever a knockback or its spawn gave it. `speed_limit_settle` caps a dynamic body's `Velocity` again after the solver, so it also leaves every step at or under the cap. Inside a step the solver adds gravity and the contacts' impulses (A34), so only a dynamic body in free fall, touching nothing and pushed by nothing, has a bound on its move: at most `(cap + g·dt)·dt` a step.
- Edges: `speed_limit` after `path_follow`, `linear_motion`, `oscillator`, both controllers, `steering`, `follow`, `orbit` and `knockback`; `speed_limit_settle` none.
- Recipes: platformer (the acrobat falls at its cap), shmup (an accelerating drifter tops out). Rows: speed, x, y. Bench: `speed_limit`, bodiless, and `speed_limit_settle`, dynamic bodies.

**`Wrap`** · `wrap` · `WrapPlugin` · `WRAP` in `Overlap`
- Data: `{ "region": [[0, 0], [1280, 720]] }` or `{ "view": 0 }`, exactly one, as `despawn_outside` takes them; `size` `[0, 0]`, the entity's box from its point; `axes` one of `both` (default), `x`, `y`.
- After the step, an entity whose box has wholly left the area past one side moves by the area's extent to the other. The view is the last frame's camera view without its shake (A12, Q9), grown by the margin; the crate-private `motion/area.rs` (step 2) holds the region or view and the unshaken view for `Wrap` and `StayInView`.
- Writes a teleport (§2.1). Recipes: shmup (an asteroid wraps the arena), top-down (a rover runs through a side tunnel). Rows: area, size, axes; a region drawn. Bench: `wrap`, region.

**`StayInView`** · `stay_in_view` · `StayInViewPlugin` · `STAY_IN_VIEW` in `Overlap`
- Data: `{ "view": 0 }` or `{ "region": [[0, 0], [640, 480]] }`, exactly one; `size` `[0, 0]`.
- After the step, clamps the entity's box inside the area, the view shrunk by the margin, and zeroes the outward part of its `Velocity`, and of a platformer controller's own velocity, on a clamped axis.
- Writes a clamp (§2.1). Recipes: shmup (a fighter held against the view's edge stays in), grid (a warden stays in its room). Rows: area, size; a region drawn. Bench: `stay_in_view`, region.

**`Aim`** · `aim` · `AimPlugin` · `AIM` in `Intent`
- Data: `{ "from": "intent", "target": { "nearest": { "all": ["team.player"], "range": 320 } }, "offset": [0, 0] }`; `from` one of `intent` (default), `cursor`, `movement`, `target`; `target` required with `from: target`, accepted and unused otherwise; `offset` `[0, 0]`, the aim's origin from the point.
- Each step, a unit direction: the `Intent`'s aim; toward the cursor's world point (A16); the `Intent`'s movement, else the velocity's direction; or toward the nearest target in range. It keeps its last direction while the source gives none, so a released stick keeps aiming; `direction()` is `None` until the first. Its readers: `Facing`, C's projectiles, E's abilities.
- Writes its own state only. Edges: after `action_input`.
- Recipes: top-down (a sentry aims at the nearest hero in range and holds when it leaves), shmup (a gun aims at the ship). Rows: source, direction, target; a ray drawn. Bench: `aim`, one target.

**`Facing`** · `facing` · `FacingPlugin` · `FACING` in `Present`
- Data: `{ "from": "motion", "snap": "free", "turn": false, "offset": 0, "min_speed": 1 }`; `from` one of `motion` (default), `intent`, `aim`; `snap` one of `free` (default), `horizontal`, `four`, `eight`; `turn` false; `offset` finite radians, 0; `min_speed` at least 0, 1.
- Each frame, its direction from the velocity (a `Velocity`, else the last step's move over the step), the `Intent`'s movement or the `Aim`'s direction; held below `min_speed` or with no input, so a stopped character keeps facing; snapped, `horizontal` keeping the last side while it moves vertically. With `turn`, writes `Transform.rotation` as the direction's angle plus `offset` (art drawn facing up turns with `offset` π/2). It flips nothing (Q1). Works on children.
- Recipes: platformer (the acrobat faces its run), top-down (a seeker faces four ways), shmup (a gun turns to its aim). Rows: source, direction. Bench: `facing`.

**`CharacterAnimator`** · `character_animator` · `CharacterAnimatorPlugin` · `CHARACTER_ANIMATOR` in `Present`; `Clips`
- Data: `{ "idle": "hero_idle", "move": "hero_run", "rise": "hero_jump", "fall": "hero_fall", "min_speed": 10 }`; each clip a manifest animation ID or a set by facing, `{ "left": "…", "right": "…", "up": "…", "down": "…" }`, a set naming `left` and `right`, `up` and `down` falling back to the last side; `idle` required, `move`, `rise` and `fall` falling back to `idle`; `min_speed` at least 0, 10. - Each frame, a state: `rise` or `fall` while a platformer controller is off the ground, by its velocity's sign; `move` at `min_speed` or faster; else `idle` (Q12). The clip is the state's, or its set's entry for the entity's `Facing` by dominant axis, else its velocity's side. On a change of clip, after `animation_tick`, it replaces the `AnimationState` and writes the new clip's first frame into `Sprite.asset_id`, so the frame that selects a clip shows its first frame however short (A36), and the clip advances from the next frame. Works on children.
- Writes `AnimationState`, and `Sprite.asset_id` on a change. Edges: after `facing` and `animation_tick`.
- Recipes: platformer (the acrobat idles, runs either way, rises and falls), top-down (a seeker walks four ways and idles). Rows: state, clip. Bench: `character_animator`.

**`DepthSort`** · `depth_sort` · `DepthSortPlugin` · `DEPTH_SORT` in `Present`
- Data: `{ "offset": 32, "base": 0 }`; `offset` finite, 0, the feet's distance below the drawn point, such as the sprite's height; `base` an `i32`, 0.
- Each frame, `Sprite.z_order = base + round(Transform.position.y + offset)`, clamped to `i32`, so what stands lower draws in front; the extract sorts by `(z_order, entity)` as ever. Works on children.
- Recipes: top-down (a seeker draws in front of a crate above it and behind one below), grid (crawlers sort as they pass). Rows: offset, base, z-order. Bench: `depth_sort`.
