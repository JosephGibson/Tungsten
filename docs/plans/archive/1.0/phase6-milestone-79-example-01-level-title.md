## 2. Design

### 2.1 The picker

Three cards in a row, one per difficulty, under the "Difficulty" heading, with Back under them at the title's button width, centred. Each card is the difficulty's button, so it focuses, hovers and presses as a button does, keeps its caption as its accessible name ("Easy", "Normal", "Hard") and takes the theme's focus border: its name in the heading family at `HEADING_SIZE` on `HEADING_LINE`, its rank as three squares of `theme::PIP` (10) in the accent colour, filled up to its place (Easy one, Hard three), the rest outlined in the border colour, and the game's note split at " · ", a fact a line, muted, centred, in one column with no gap. The labels stretch to the card's width with centred text, never sized to their own text (the known issue `D-187` names), so nothing wraps out of sight. The cards share a row at `theme::WIDE_WIDTH` (600), each `Sizing::Fill`, stretched to the tallest; the pages' column sizes to the shown page, so the main page keeps its 220 and its place. Normal keeps the focus when the page opens. New constants: `theme::CARD_PADDING` (12) and `theme::PIP`.

`difficulty_note`'s doc in both games and the guide's line: short facts joined by " · ", each a line on its card, which `fit.rs` checks at text 200 %. The seam is unchanged. Prototype (scratch copy): 7 of the template's 8 picker and fit tests pass, the eighth being the old note-beside test this step replaces; the main page's capture is byte-identical.

### 2.2 The kit's gaps

- **K16, a push inside the window.** `ContactDamage::with_push_always(bool)` and `push_always()`; the scene field `"push_always": true`, false by default and written only when true, so every saved or hashed form of today's contact damage is unchanged. Its hits' `DamageRequest`s carry a crate-private flag, read where the request resolves (round 1, finding 2): a request ignored because its target is inside its window (alive, not dead, not despawning) still lands its knockback through `knock_back`, with no damage, flash or event; a landed hit pushes as today, once; a killing one pushes nothing, as today. A contact of no damage queues no request, so with the flag it pushes at the touch. `DamageRequest`'s public surface is unchanged. Rustdoc: a lava pool that throws whatever falls in, hurting only outside the window.
- **K17, the ground.** `PlatformerController::ground() -> Option<Entity>`: the body it stood on at its last step's end, `None` in the air or on tiles. Kept from the step's ground probe or its floor slide; left out of the saved form (`#[serde(skip)]`), so a loaded controller reports none until its first step, as it takes a still first step.
- Each gets a test in its module (`overlap::contact::tests`, `motion::platformer::tests`), a [break ledger](../../1.0/w04-api-freeze.md#break-ledger) row as an addition, `just api`, and `DESIGN.md`'s two kit paragraphs (the controller, the pipeline's knockback). No inspector row unless set, so the kit hash holds.

### 2.4 The crust

- **Setting.** Each cold spray ray that crosses a basin's surface box in a step (the douse path's `fires_crossed`, widened to basins) adds the step to the basin's `cold`, which drains at 1 a second unsprayed; at `CRUST_COLD` 0.5 s the whole basin sets: `LavaCrust { left: CRUST_SECS (6.0) }`, its touch and its frost child's held as `Doused` holds a fire's, its surface body in the tiles' layer 0 with every mask, so the player, enemies and balls stand on stone; a hiss (`ex10_lava_hiss`, new) and steam bursts along the surface; the glow dims.
