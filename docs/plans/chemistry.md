## 2. Design

### 2.1 Chemistry (Step 1, the saved work)

The stopped plan's §2.3 with its seven round-1 and four round-2 fixes, as the saved work builds it:

- **Where:** a new `chemistry` domain (`mod.rs`, `rules.rs`, `queue.rs`, `outputs.rs`; the redo's Q11). `ChemistryPlugin` joins `KitPlugins::set()` after `EffectsPlugin`; its system `chemistry` runs in Resolve after `damage_resolve` and `ailment_feed` when present, and `effect_resolve` runs after it when present, so the statuses it applies land in its step. It returns before any query while the World holds no `interactions` entry and no queued entry (A8).
- **Rules:** the `interactions` kind, a rule an entry: `{ "stimulus", "on", "min", "outputs" }`. `stimulus` is a damage type or a declared tag; the kit declares `stimulus.impact`, `stimulus.light` and `stimulus.blast` in `STIMULI` beside `KIT_TAGS` (A5). `on` is a `TagFilter` on the receiver's tags (its properties and its statuses' grants and bands); `min` the least amount. For a stimulus and a receiver the most specific passing rule runs alone: the deepest stimulus name, then the most dotted segments in `on`'s `all`, then the lowest entry ID. The load checks every tag, status, prefab and handler a rule names.
- **Stimuli:** each non-periodic `DamagedPart` since the last run, one per deepest type some rule answers, its amount after the resistances, credited to its source.
- **Queue,** saved as `chemistry_queue`: a stimulus joins only when some rule names its name or an ancestor's, one entry per stimulus and receiver. A merge sums the raw amounts (`min` reads them) and, apart, each hit's amount times its status effect (a `scale` build reads that), in `f64` clamped to `f32::MAX`, and keeps the largest status effect and DoT base, the least depth, the first credit and the entry's age. Past the bound (16,384) an add is dropped and counted, warned of once a run. A run takes at most the cap (256) of the entries queued before it, oldest first (age, depth, the receiver's save order, the stimulus). Each decides as it runs: nothing for a receiver gone or despawning, or when no rule passes; a dead receiver reacts with `build` and `apply` refused and `remove` and `consume` run, and the kit's despawn holds an entity while an entry queued before its despawn came due waits, the held entity taking no new entry (A10). An output that stimulates queues at depth + 1; past the depth limit (8) it is dropped and counted (`cut`); a reaction's damage carries its depth onto its parts (`DamagedPart::depth`, the request's flag byte, A5), so a cycle through damage ends. `with_cap`, `with_bound`, `with_depth`; `Chemistry::pending()`, `dropped()`, `cut()`, `ran()`. The settings live apart from the saved queue, in an unsaved resource the plugin writes, since a load replaces the saved resource whole (A21): a world keeps its own cap, bound, depth and (Step 2) light period through a load (round 1's finding 1). The first run after a load cuts the entries past its depth limit, then drops the youngest past its bound, each counted, so a queue restored from looser settings meets the receiving world's before it runs (round 2's finding 2); an unsaved flag the saved form leaves unset marks that run, so a saturated queue is not walked every step.
- **Outputs,** in order on the receiver: `build` (a status by `amount`, or `scale` × the weighted sum), `apply` (a status with `dot` and `lines`), each naming its request's status effect; `remove`; `consume`; `damage` (typed parts on the receiver from the credit, resolved at the next step's `damage_resolve`); `spawn` (a prefab at the receiver's point, from it); `handler` (`with_handler`; the load refuses an unregistered name). A status's `on_end` (run out, decayed, fuel spent, removed or ended, or an instant effect's landing, which expires it at once, A21) and a band's `enter` (on first reaching it from below) queue their outputs at depth 0 from a tick and at the request's depth from an application, which an entry's output makes its depth + 1, credited to the instance's or the request's source (round 1's finding 6). An instant effect's `on_expire` request carries the request's depth as its `on_end` does, so a cycle through `on_expire` deepens and ends at the depth limit; a timed expiry's, from a tick, starts at depth 0 (A23; round 2's finding 1).
- **PA-8's rates:** `DamagedPart` gains `dot_base`, `status_effect` and `depth`. A sheet's part's DoT base is its drawn amount before the factors and the crit, times its `delivery.dot` sums with their deferred lines, times the skill's status effect at the use (`Delivery::status_effect`; the ARPG design's §6.4, step 7); a plain part's is its amount before the factors times its source's `status_effect`. `apply`'s `dot: s` fixes the instance's rate at `s × dot_base` a second; its ticks deal `rate × period` shared over the effect's damage types in their data's proportions, whatever the effect's scale curve or intensity reads (the payload factor, which scales a periodic payload, leaves a fixed rate: round 1's finding 2, A21), periodic (§6.5: no range or crit draw), typed at the application so the source's later conversion leaves them (A11); a re-application's rate replaces it (Q13).
- **Trace:** each output writes a `KitTrace` line naming its rule, in today's kinds (`Skipped`, `Cue`, `Spawn`, `SpawnFailed`), so no trace kind is new.
- **Recipes:** top-down `wooden_crate` (`material.wood`) lit into `ablaze` by fire and doused by cold; platformer `kindling` given `smoulder` at half a torch's hit a second, credited to the torch.

### 2.2 Spread, impacts and light (Step 2)

- **Emissions:** an effect's `emit: { "stimulus", "amount", "radius", "period" }` (each finite, the last three above 0): every `period` of an instance's life, on its own clock counted as its period is (A9) and saved with it, skipped at its default, its holder emits `stimulus` of `amount` × the instance's factor at its point to every receiver within `radius` but itself, at depth 0, credited to the instance's source. `effect_tick` pushes each into a kept buffer only while the World holds `Chemistry`, and the next `chemistry` run drains it, so no query is added (A8). The output `{ "emit": { "stimulus", "amount", "radius" } }` emits once from an entry at its depth + 1, credited to its credit.
- **Receivers in a radius:** each entity with `Tags` and a point: a body's `Position` plus its `Collider`'s offset, measured to its surface (`Shape::distance`), else its `Transform`'s translation, measured to the point; `d ≤ radius` reaches. A grid of 64 px cells holds every entity with `Tags` or `Health` and a point, so Step 5's blast finds its targets without tags in it too (round 1's finding 3); a stimulus reaches only those with `Tags`. It is built from kept buffers once a run, only in a run with radius work (emissions, blasts), and its candidates are tested exactly.
- **`chemistry_tick`:** a second system, in Tick after `effect_tick` and `ailment_tick` when present, idle under `chemistry`'s rule. It registers the impacts' queue and samples the light here, and from Steps 3 and 4 ticks tile statuses and `Became`. kit.rs's snapshot, `EFFECT_EDGES` (`(EFFECT_TICK, CHEMISTRY_TICK)`, `(AILMENT_TICK, CHEMISTRY_TICK)`) with `build_owner`, and the template's `SCHEDULE` take it.
- **Impacts:** with a rule loaded whose stimulus is `stimulus.impact` or an ancestor of it, `chemistry_tick` calls `World::register_event::<ContactBegan>()` before the step's physics, since the record runs only for a step that starts with the queue (A12), so a world's first step counts. `chemistry` reads the step's begins with its own cursor: each stimulates `a` and `b` (when an entity) by the closing speed, credited to the other's `Source` entity, else the other; a tile credits none. A world without such a rule never registers the queue.
- **Light:** with a rule whose stimulus is `stimulus.light` or an ancestor of it, every 0.25 s on the fixed clock (`with_light_period`), `chemistry_tick` gathers the lights once and samples each receiver some light rule's `on` passes: a `stimulus.light` of the luma of the sum of `light_from` over the lights (A14).

### 2.3 Tiles (Step 3)

- **Cells as receivers:** a cell whose tile has a `material` (A15); its tags are the material's name read as a tag (a name that is no valid tag gives none) and its tile statuses' grants and bands (Q8). A cell is a placement's: its map ID with its `TilemapInstance`'s origin, by the origin's bits as the physics keys a placement (`physics/step/gather.rs:762-764`), then its layer, column and row, since two placements of one map may stand apart and an area's map carries no save identity (A22; round 1's finding 5). A radius stimulus reaches each placement's cells, on every layer, whose centres lie within it; an impact on a tile stimulates its cell, its placement read from `TileHit::map`'s `TilemapInstance`. The queue's receiver is an entity or a cell; a cell's entry saves its key in a `cell` field, an entity's as in Step 1, and cells order after entities by map ID, origin, layer, row and column.
- **Tile statuses,** a saved resource (`tile_statuses`), sparse by that key: an effect's intensity, decay, bands, duration, period, `emit` and `on_end` apply; its lines, resistances, heal, restore and periodic damage do nothing on a cell. `chemistry_tick` ticks them; the statuses of a placement no `TilemapInstance` holds, gone or moved, drop at its tick (Q17).
- **Outputs on a cell:** the status outputs; `emit` and `spawn` from its centre, the spawn with no source; `damage` as the sum of its parts through `damage_tile`; `set_tile`: `{ "set_tile": "<sprite ID>", "revert": seconds }` through `TilemapRegistry::set_tile` at the first tileset index with that sprite (a sprite the map lacks does nothing, traced), which writes the map's data that every placement of its ID shows (A15); `handler`. A revert keeps the old tile by sprite ID, as saves write cells, and whether the cell was an auto cell (`TilemapData::is_auto`), since writing a resolved variant back would make it an override that later retiles skip (A22; round 1's finding 4): an auto cell reverts to its style's brush (the first tileset tile whose `TileProps` has the old tile's style and is a brush), which `set_tile` resolves and marks auto again, any other to its old sprite. Reverts are kept per map cell (map ID, layer, column, row), the level the tile data is shared at, with the sprite their write wrote, since `set_tile` writes whatever the cell holds (A23; round 2's finding 3): a later `set_tile` output on the cell, from any placement, replaces the pending revert, keeping its old tile when it reverts too, so overlapping reverts end on the original, and dropping it when it does not; a revert running out writes back only while the registry's edit record still holds its write for the cell (`edited_cells`), so a game's write, a broken cell or another edit since drops it, traced. They save with the tile statuses.

### 2.4 `become` keeping identity (Step 4)

- **Core:** `CommandBuffer::replace_prefab(entity, prepared) -> PendingEntity` beside `spawn_prefab_from`: it queues the prefab's spawn and a `Command::Replace { source, target }` of `Derive`'s shape, the prefab's ID through `SavedPrefab`'s queue as `DeriveSaved` carries it, so `Command` stays 24 bytes and `CommandBuffer` gains no field (A5). At the flush, with `entity` alive: the spawn takes the spawn inheritance from it as `spawn_prefab_from`'s does (A16), then its save identity in place of a new one: `Saved`'s ID and area with the new prefab's ID as its prefab, when the prefab is saved; then `entity` despawns. A dead `entity` drops the spawn whole, as a dead source does.
- **What a capture writes:** a named entity replaced reads as gone by its name, and the replacement as a spawn record of the new prefab under the authored ID (A16), a run's spawn replaced as one under its ID; a restore despawns the gone first and makes the replacement once. A reference by save ID resolves to the replacement after a load; one by `Entity` ends, as to any despawned entity.
- **Kit:** the output `{ "become": "<prefab>", "revert": { "after": seconds, "to": "<prefab>" } }` at the receiver's point. The replacement keeps the receiver's `Source` when it has one (a `with` bundle, which the derivation leaves) and otherwise takes the derivation's, naming the receiver, as any spawn from an entity since despawned does (Q9). With `revert`, it holds `Became` (saved as `became`: `to` and the time left) and becomes `to` under the same identity when the time runs out, `chemistry_tick` counting it. The data names `to`, since a named entity's `Saved` holds no prefab. Statuses are not carried (Q9).

### 2.5 The blast (Step 5)
