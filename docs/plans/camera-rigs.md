## 2. Design

### 2.1 The camera's output and rigs in core (Step 1)

- **`CameraState`**, the drawn view (top-left `position`, `zoom`, `rotation`), gains `shake: Vec2`, the offset the update added, and `unshaken()`, the view less it. Only `camera_update_system` writes it, each frame from the winning rig, and it never reads `position` or `zoom` back: `resolve_base_*`, `record_output_*` and `CameraMode` go (A1). `rotation` stays the game's: the update keeps it and the clamp reads it (Q8). Without the update (02's rows, extract tests) a write lasts until an update runs.
- **`CameraRig`** (`Copy`; a component and the base rig's type): `kind` (`RigKind::Follow(FollowRig)`, `Fixed(FixedRig)` or `Scripted(Vec2)`), `bounds: Option<CameraBounds>`, `view_height: Option<f32>` (world units top to bottom; none: the window's height at zoom 1), `zoom` (a scale over the view, 1), `priority: i32` (0), `blend: RigBlend` (`time` 0, which cuts; `ease` `Easing::SineInOut`), and its raise state: live (by default) and the stamp of its raise (2.2). Builders, `raise`, `lower`, `is_live` and `set_point`, and the World-level `camera::raise(world, entity)` and `camera::lower(world, entity)`, which stamp from the controller (2.2); constructors refuse non-finite and out-of-range values with `#[track_caller]` panics.
- **`FollowRig`**: `target: Option<Entity>` (none: the rig's own entity; on the base rig, the target `camera_target` names, else nothing, which holds), `offset` (added to the target's drawn point: the anchor), `dead_zone` (world units, centred, clamped to the view), `smoothing: Vec2` (`D-100`'s rate per 1/60 s, per axis; 1 by default), `lead: Option<RigLead>`.
- **`RigLead`**: `from` (`Velocity`: the target's `Velocity`, else its drawn point's change over the frame; `Aim`: the direction `CameraAim` gives), `max` (px), `time` (seconds of velocity ahead) and `smoothing` (per 1/60 s); its goal is `v · time` or `direction · max`, capped at `max`, which the lead eases toward.
- **`FixedRig`**: `At(centre)`, `Corner(top-left)` or `Rect(bounds)`, centred on the rect at the rig's view height, else the least that shows it whole at the window's aspect. **`Scripted(point)`**: the view's centre, which a game or a later timeline writes.
- **`CameraController`** stays `Copy`: `rig`, the base rig (live under the rest, at its priority, raised first; by default a follow with no target, which holds, so a default World still draws its top-left at the origin at zoom 1); the shake's fields; `shake_scale` (1); private runtime (the winner, the blend, the winner's follow state, the last view, the stamp counter, the frame's summary). `winner()` (`None` for the base), `frame()` (the last update's view, dead zone, bounds, anchor and lead), `cut_to(centre)` (a cut the next update takes: any blend ends and the winner starts its state there, at its own height), `add_trauma`, and `shake_offset()` times `shake_scale`.
- **`CameraAim(fn(&World, Entity) -> Option<Vec2>)`**: the source an `Aim` lead reads, which the kit inserts (2.4); without one an aim lead stays at zero.

### 2.2 The update (Step 1)

1. **Candidates:** the base rig and, while `World::has_any::<CameraRig>()`, each live entity rig through one `query_filtered::<Entity, With<CameraRig>>()` a frame, read with `get`. The winner has the highest priority, then the latest stamp (the base's is 0), then the lower entity index. A raise is stamped when it happens: core's `camera::raise(world, entity)` takes the controller's next stamp, so the kit's zones and cue actions order their raises as they make them, two in one step included. A rig live from its spawn, or raised in Rust by `CameraRig::raise`, takes a stamp at the next update, in query order, written back through `query_mut::<&mut CameraRig>()` on that frame alone; a lowered rig loses its stamp.
2. **The winner's view** (corner, height), each rig clamped to its own bounds at its own height. A rig starts its state at the last view when it wins or its kind changes (that view's corner when the heights match, else its centre; Q5), and the winner at a pending `cut_to`'s centre, winning or not, so a follow steps on from where the camera stood, by A3's arithmetic per axis, its lead added to the anchor before the dead zone, and one with no target holds. Fixed and scripted rigs take their point.
3. **Zoom:** `(window height / view height) × zoom`, the view height the window's when unset. 01's `(height / 1152) × Zoom` and the template's `height / 720` keep their floats, and with A3's arithmetic kept, a rig that restates today's camera draws today's frames.
4. **Blends:** when the winner changes, the incoming rig has a blend time and a view was drawn before, the update keeps that last drawn view, unshaken, as the blend's start, fixed for the blend, and each frame draws it moved toward the incoming rig's live view by `s = ease(elapsed / time)`, `elapsed` counted by the game's dt: the centre by `start + (live - start) · s`, the height geometrically, `start · (live / start)^s`, which stays positive under the eases that overshoot (`back_*`, `bounce_*`); from `elapsed ≥ time` the rig's view alone. A time of 0, or no view before, cuts; a switch mid-blend keeps the view that frame drew as its new start. So the drawn view is a function of the time since the switch, not of the frame rate. A pause holds the blend's progress, not its endpoint: under dt 0 the view stays put unless the incoming rig's live view moves (a scripted point a game writes, a smoothing-1 follow whose target moves under the pause, `D-100`), and then it moves by the frozen weight's share of that move; freezing the view instead would jump at the resume.
5. **Shake** after: `shake_offset()` added to the corner and recorded in `shake`; the phase advances as today. Then the world anchors (A5).
- **A pause** (dt 0): a blend's progress holds (item 4), a follow holds unless its smoothing is 1 (`D-100`), the shake shows the phase it entered with (`DESIGN.md`'s pause rule).
- **Cost:** nothing allocated with no entity rig; with some, the one query's slice list a frame, its shape's regrowth after an archetype is added, and the stamp pass only on a frame a rig newly goes live (A16, 2.10).
