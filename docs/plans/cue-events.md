## 2. Design

### 2.1 01's own air-jump event (Step 1)

- `Sound::AirJumped`'s cue event becomes `double_jumped` (`components.rs:379`), its doc "The courier's free air jump, the double jump", and its rule's `on` with it in the content source and its copy (A8). The variant, its sends, its place in `Sound::ALL` and the rule's index stay: the same sound plays at the same step and point, 01's tests read the same `Sounded` events, the rule's limit keys (`<entry>.<index>`) hold, and no save names a cue event.
- Why 01's and not the kit's: the kit's names are the controller's own getters' (`jumped()`, `air_jumped()`), settled in the looks-and-cues plan over two critique rounds, and a demo's event name is the cheaper thing to move. 7.13 moves this sound onto the kit's `air_jumped` with `amount: { "max": 0 }` and drops `double_jumped`. The decision records the rule: a kit cue event's name is reserved as the kit adds it, and a game in this repository holding it is renamed in the same change (Q1, Q2).

### 2.2 Named game events (Step 2, S-39)

- `CueEvent` gains `name: Option<&'static str>` and `named(name)`. `CuesOnEvent::with_names(names, map)` registers `E` once under each of `names`, each checked as `new`'s name is (A1), and `with_names(&[])` panics. Its reader runs an occurrence under the name it carries and skips one that carries none of its names, or no name, logging once per carried name.
- `new(name, map)` stays: an occurrence it reads may carry no name or its own; another is skipped and logged. Its `Debug` keeps `name` (`tests/cues.rs:468-472`) and `with_names`' shows `names`. Each name may carry an amount and an other, as `new`'s does.

### 2.3 The controller's cue events (Step 2, S-48)

- Four kit cue events, each about the controller's entity at its feet (its collider's bottom centre, with its offset), with no other: `jumped`, a ground or coyote jump, where its step began; `air_jumped`, an air jump, where its step began, its amount the stat amount it paid, 0 for a free one; `jump_refused`, a fresh press in the air the cost refused (`air_jump_refused()`), where its step began; `landed`, below, where the step ended. `KIT_EVENTS` and `CueEventNames` grow from 13 to 17; `air_jumped` and `landed` carry an amount and none an other or kinds, so the load refuses `amount` on `jumped` and `jump_refused`, and `target: other` and `kind` on all four (A3).
- **A landing, changed from the patch (round 1).** The patch read a landing as grounded at a step's end, in the air at the last one's, and falling as the step began, its amount that starting speed, so a fall from rest that starts and ends within one step was no landing at dt 1/30 and one at dt 1/60 (A4b). Here a step lands when it ends grounded after a step that ended in the air and its ground-finding stopped a downward speed: the speed the controller had when its start-of-step check found the floor, or the speed after the step's gravity when its move met the floor, whichever grounded it. The amount is that speed in px/s, which `Moved` carries to the runner. A controller spawned standing, grounded by a check at rest, stops no speed and does not land; a loaded one's first step checks no ground (A4).
- `PlatformerController::landed()` joins the step's flags, reset as a step starts and set at its end. It is not saved, unlike its siblings (round 1): a field a landing step's save adds would make that save corrupt to the build before (A4c), and a loaded controller's first step clears every step flag without moving (A4), so the flag would only show between a load and that step. The controller's saved form keeps its bytes.
- The runner pushes a step's events to `ControllerCues`, a kit list `CuesPlugin` inserts with room for 32; the cue system drains it after the kit's queues and before the game's (after `LevelUp`, `cues.rs:3555-3560`). No `EventQueue`, so no `events_sent` (Q4). A World without `CuesPlugin` has no list and pushes nothing.
- **New here: sent only when named.** Before it pushes an event, the runner asks `cue_named(world, entity, event)`, a `pub(crate)` read in `react/cues.rs`: the entity's own `cues` list an action for it, or a loaded `cue_rules` rule is on it, any rule, its filters read later as for any occurrence. The read walks the entity's lists and the rules' `on` names and allocates nothing (`DataRegistry::ids` is an iterator, core `assets/data.rs:222-228`); it runs once per event a step would push, so only on jump, refusal and landing steps, against at most 01's 91 rules. A World that names none of the four (01's after Step 1, the template's, 02's) pushes nothing, so the cue system keeps its early return (A2) and 01's pit holds whatever its foes do in the window (A9), where the stopped patch would have run the cue system for each foe's landing there unmeasured (A12). The flags and `landed()` are set whether or not anything names the events (Q3).
