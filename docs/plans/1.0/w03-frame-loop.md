## Context digest

- W3 gives the frame its clocks: a `Time` resource with real and game clocks, scale, pause, elapsed time and a frame index, a plain `Timer`, and a bounded fixed-step accumulator that runs `fixed_update` zero or more times a frame with render interpolation. The stages are W15a's (`D-128`); W3 adds none.
- Today one `DeltaTime { dt }` resource (`crates/tungsten-core/src/time.rs`) carries the frame's elapsed time capped at 0.1 s (`D-088`), pinned to 1/60 s in smoke runs and written as given by the harness (`D-110`). Tweens, particles, game feel, the camera, state transitions and `physics_step` read it; `physics_step` splits it across substeps, each call advancing at most `max_step_dt` (1/30 s, `D-094`). There is no pause, time scale, frame index or timer type, and `sync_position_to_transform` copies `Position` into `Transform` after the step.
- The spike and the gate fixed the shape (`D-129`): inside a step `DeltaTime.dt` and `Time::delta()` read the fixed step, outside it the game dt; `InputState` answers edges from a fixed view inside the steps; at most 2 steps a frame, whole steps beyond the bound dropped; a paused game runs no step; `PrevPosition` is spawned with the body, never adopted, since inserting it moves the body's archetype and reorders the solve (`D-066`); `physics_sync` interpolates in `post_update`, so the extract and `D-018` are untouched; transitions, the HUD and UI on real time.
- At the pinned 1/60 s a frame runs one step with `alpha` 0: the digests, which hash `Position`, and the physics hash hold, while a body with history renders the previous step's position, one step (16.7 ms) behind, which a pixel test of a moving body sees.
- Track C runs W15a, W3a, W15b, W3b, W15c, then the Phase 5 QA pass (amendment 18). W3b extends W15b's `RigidBodyBundle` with `PrevPosition`; gameplay that drives bodies opts into `fixed_update`, a rule W15a writes into the template and the guide.

## Scope

Moved verbatim from [criteria](criteria.md) §6 on 2026-10-06; section references now name their files. Implementation plan amendment 7 was already in the design bullet. Folded in, as italic notes after the bullets they touch: the frame-loop gate's decisions (`D-129` on every bullet; `D-128` on the shape, design and open bullets; `D-131` and `D-102` on the design bullet). Amendments 16 and 18 touch the candidates rather than a bullet, so they are in the [steps](#steps).

- `D-088` and `D-094` park it here. A fixed-step accumulator with render interpolation changes the core/render seam, because the extract needs previous transforms (`D-088`). *Decided otherwise at the frame-loop gate (`D-129`): interpolation is the sync stage's, so the seam does not change. `PrevPosition` holds each body's position before the step and the interpolating `physics_sync` in `post_update` writes `Transform`, so the extract, the renderer and `D-018` are untouched and `Position` stays the true state (`D-033`). `D-129` amends `D-088` and `D-094`.*
- **Proposed shape:** physics, and opt-in gameplay systems, at a fixed step; a bound on steps per frame that replaces or wraps `max_step_dt`; the extract interpolates `Transform` between the last two steps. Smoke runs and benchmarks pin 1/60 s, so at a 60 Hz step they see one step per frame and should keep their digests. *Decided (`D-128`, `D-129`): physics and gameplay that opts in run in `fixed_update`, before `update`; `max_steps_per_frame` (default 2, which is `D-094`'s 1/30 s of simulated time a frame at the default step) bounds the loop and drops whole steps beyond it, so below 30 FPS the game runs slow instead of spiralling, and `max_step_dt` stays the step's own invariant for direct callers; `physics_sync` interpolates between `PrevPosition` and `Position` by `alpha`, not the extract. The spike kept smoke, the physics hash and every digest at the pin, one step a frame with `alpha` 0.*
- **What it unlocks:** the deep-pile load limit's lever (`contact_hertz`) needs a constant substep ([known issues](../../known-issues.md#recorded-limits)); frame-rate-independent gameplay; replays. *Frame-rate-independent gameplay is gameplay that opts into `fixed_update` and reads the fixed edge view, so a press reaches exactly one step at every frame rate (`D-129`). The `contact_hertz` lever is a [follow-up](#follow-ups) once W3b's step is constant; replays are not built (`D-129`).*
- **Game clock (owner-added).** Proposed: a `Time` resource in place of the single `dt`, holding real time (capped as `D-088` caps today's dt), game time (scaled by `time_scale`, zero while `paused`), elapsed time on both clocks, a frame index and, with the fixed step, the step size, the steps run this frame and the interpolation fraction. `DeltaTime` feeds tweens, particles, game feel, the camera, state transitions and the physics step today, and each must pick a clock: game time for simulation and animation, real time for UI, transitions, the HUD and hit-stop, so a pause menu animates over a frozen world. Every engine stage must treat a zero game step as a no-op. Smoke runs and benchmarks pin dt at scale 1 and should keep their digests. `DeltaTime.dt` stays as the game dt until the freeze ([w04](w04-api-freeze.md)). A paused clock freezes motion, not input: the routed gameplay view (UI M3) keeps gameplay systems from acting on input under a pause menu. *Decided as proposed (`D-129`), with the step 1/60 s by default and the dropped time on the clock too. Inside a step `DeltaTime.dt` and `Time::delta()` read the fixed step and outside it the game dt, so each engine stage reads the dt its stage hands it, and `InputState` answers edge queries from a fixed view inside the steps. The clock table (question 13): `physics_step` and opt-in gameplay on the fixed step; tweens, particles, game feel, the camera and game systems in `update` on the game clock; state transitions, the HUD, overlays, the inspector, UI animation tracks, navigation repeat and double-click timing on real time, transitions amending `D-093`. W3a deprecates `DeltaTime`, and W4b removes it.*
- **Timers.** A plain `Timer` value (duration, elapsed, once or repeating), owned by a component or resource and ticked with whichever clock its owner picks. `tick(dt)` returns how many times it finished, so a long frame cannot swallow a repeat. No timer system or event queue in the first version. *Decided as proposed (`D-129`). W13's lifetime and despawn-after timers build on it.*
- **Design together:** W1's input-routing stages, W2's render hand-off point (R4), W15's game-facing stages and plugins ([w15](w15-authoring-api.md)) and telemetry semantics all edit the same stage list. Agree one frame-loop decision set at the frame-loop gate, before UI M3 and W15a; UI M0 does not wait for it (implementation plan amendment 7), and R4's hand-off point joins it only if R4 comes back from 1.x (`D-102`). *Agreed at the frame-loop gate on 2026-10-06 as one set (`D-128`, `D-129`): W15's five closed stages are W3's stage map, with `fixed_update` before `update`; W1 M3's `ui_route` sits in `pre_update` after the state dispatcher, once a frame on the real clock; a "frame" stays the render frame for telemetry. R4 stays in 1.x (`D-102`), and no worker runs inside a step (`D-131`).*
- **Open:** physics only, or a fixed-update schedule for systems? Interpolation on by default, or opt-in per entity? What the HUD and telemetry call a "frame" once update and render rates differ. Which clock camera shake and particles follow during hit-stop? Does a paused game skip the physics stage or step it by zero? *Answered at the frame-loop gate (`D-128`, `D-129`): a `fixed_update` stage that takes any system, with physics as one plugin's pair in it (question 7); interpolation on by default for every body spawned with `PrevPosition`, switchable globally (question 7); a "frame" stays the render frame for the HUD and telemetry, the fixed steps a count on it and each system's runs summed under its name in the `systems:` line; camera shake and particles follow the game clock, so they freeze under hit-stop with everything else, and a game that wants shake over real time reads the real clock; a paused game skips the physics stage (question 13).*

## Follow-ups

W3b provides the step; moving the default changes the physics hash and the engine's piles, so it is a decision of its own with no candidate yet. W3b's plan gave it a [backlog](backlog-1.x.md) row (M41 Q8).
2. **Cursor and scroll deltas per step.** A fixed-step reader of `cursor_delta` sees the whole frame's delta at every step and double-counts at two steps a frame; the fixed view's trick extends to deltas if such a reader turns up (`D-129`).
3. **Replays and a per-system step rate** are not decided (`D-129`) and have no [backlog](backlog-1.x.md) row; the feature gate adds one if the acceptance game asks.
4. **Workers inside a step** wait with R1 in 1.x (`D-131`).
5. **The kit's helpers** spawn bodies with `PrevPosition` and carry the teleport rule (W13, `D-129`).
