## 2. Design

### 2.1 Clips (Step 1)

- **One-shots** (P7-19): `CharacterAnimator::play(clips)` shows a clip once over the state's clip, from its first frame, for its length (its frames' durations at the entity's animation speed, counted by the game clock's delta, so a pause holds it), then the state's clip from its first frame. A play during one restarts with the new clip, the same clip included; `stop()` ends it; `playing()` names it. A one-shot wins over rules and motion. It is presentation, not saved (A2).
- **The `clip` cue action:** `{ "clip": <clips> }` plays a one-shot on the subject's animator, a traced skip without one; the load checks each ID. So 01's land, air-jump and toss clips become `landed`, `air_jumped` and `ability_used` cues (`D-243`). Cue actions become fifteen forms (A5).
- **Rules** (S-20): `character_animator`'s `rules`, a list of `clip_rule` `{ "when": <tag filter>, "motion": "idle" | "move" | "rise" | "fall", "clip": <clips> }`, each with `when` or `motion` or both; the first that passes picks the clip, else the motion state's. A state the game sets is a tag it inserts (`state.charging` by convention, as SF2's `state.dodging` is), so the rules, the looks (2.3) and the hit filters read one vocabulary. The load checks the tags and IDs.
- **Aim poses** (S-66): `Clips` gains `{ "aim": ["down", "down_fwd", "fwd", "up_fwd", "up"] }`, two or more IDs drawn facing right, from straight down to straight up: the entry nearest the elevation of the entity's `Aim` direction (else its `Facing`'s) on the side it points to, shown flipped on the left, as a `right`-only set is. An aim set serves any state, rule or one-shot, so 01's cast pose is an aim-set one-shot over its idle clip.
- **An ability's look** (S-105): `ability`'s `icon` (a manifest sprite ID, checked at load) and `clip` (clips), kept together behind an `Arc`, so an entry grows by 8 bytes and the ticker's per-step clone (A4) copies `None` or a shared pointer, allocating nothing for either, which a test holds with `Arc::ptr_eq` across a tick of an inline ability that sets both; a use plays `clip` as a one-shot on the user after its payloads; `Abilities::icon(data, name)` and `Abilities::clip(data, name)` read them for a bar through the entry's `DataRef` and the `DataRegistry` at each read, nothing cached, so an ID-backed entry (01's player's) reads before its first tick and a reload shows on the next read (`intent/ability.rs:1623-1638`, core `assets/data.rs:424-432`). Both are skipped when none, so a saved entry keeps its bytes.
- Recipes: `platformer`'s hero landing on a one-shot clip and running again; `rpg`'s warden's throw clip over its walk on `ability_used`; `top_down`'s ghoul showing its alert clip while `state.alerted` and walking; `arpg`'s caster casting `cinder` in an aim pose, its icon named.

### 2.2 Facing and hit shapes (Step 2)

- **`Facing::face(direction)`** (S-150) sets the direction, snapped by its snap and ignoring its minimum speed; a zero or non-finite one is ignored. The free function `face_toward(world, entity, point)` faces its `Facing` from the entity's point (`Position`, else `Transform`). The next present pass keeps it while the source gives nothing, so a face holds until the source turns it (A6).
- **`facing`'s `mirror`** (S-78), with `turn` only (the load refuses it alone): while the direction points left of the vertical (`x < 0`) the sprite flips and turns to `θ − π − offset`, else unflipped to `θ + offset`, `θ` the direction's angle; a straight-up or straight-down direction keeps the last side. Art drawn facing right flies right side up both ways (A7).
- **A hitbox by facing** (P7-20, K3): `hitbox`'s `facing`, `"mirror"` or `"turn"`, none by default: its offset, written for an entity facing right, follows its entity's `Facing`: `mirror` negates its x while the facing points left; `turn` rotates it by the facing's angle, and an `aabb` swaps its half extents while the facing is nearer vertical. Without a `Facing` the offset stands. One crate helper places the shape for the hit runner and the debug draw (A8). `Facing` is written once a frame, so the swing takes the side its entity faced at the last present pass; at two steps a frame both take the same side (Q3).
- **`KitDebug`** (K22): two switches, `shapes` and `trace`; `set_enabled(on)` sets both, as before, so 01's mirror and the script overlay keep their meaning; the draw reads `shapes`, the log `trace`. `KitDebug::set_key(Some(action))` names an action whose press flips `shapes` alone, read at the top of `kit_debug_draw` before its early return; none by default (Q1, A9, A10).
- Recipes: `shooter`'s missile mirrored past the vertical as it turns back; `rpg`'s warden's pole swinging ahead of it in each of four facings (`turn`, LW14); `platformer`'s knight's swing mirrored when it turns; `top_down`'s sentry facing the point it heard and holding it while still; `top_down`'s hit shapes drawn on a key with the trace off.

### 2.3 Looks by tag and the health bar (Step 3)

- **`looks`** (S-10), a scene component: a list of `tag_look` `{ "when": <tag filter>, "look": <look>, "grow": 0.4 }`: while `when` passes the entity's tags, the look's parts show as a status look's do, at a share of 1, or rising linearly from 0 over `grow` seconds of game time shown; a filter that stops passing takes its parts at the next early pass and starts its time again. An entry's offsets mirror by its holder's `Facing` (left of the vertical) when it has one, else by its drawn flip, so 01's mirrored clip sets mirror their marks. The look passes take these entries beside `LookHolders`' (A11), each part kept by its holder and entry index.
- **`look_overlay`'s `scale`**, `[from, to]`, `[1, 1]` by default: the overlay's scale by the share, so a slam's ring grows over its tell and a status overlay with its intensity.
- **`health_bar`** (S-52), a scene component: `{ "size": [64, 6], "rise": 6, "back": [0.05, 0.04, 0.05, 0.86], "fill": [0.77, 0.19, 0.16, 1.0], "inset": 1, "full": true }`: two `engine_white` sprites, the back centred over the holder's drawn centre with its bottom `rise` above the drawn top, the fill `inset` inside it from the left at the health's share; colours as a glow's; hidden while dead or, with `full: false`, at full health; drawn at the holder's depth plus 1 and 2; one warning and nothing drawn without `engine_white`. ### 2.4 At the kill: the death policy, the switches, a lethal kill; the blink (Step 4)

- **Switches** (S-156): `ContactDamage` and `Hitbox` gain `on` (true by default, skipped when true), `set_on` and `is_on`. An off contact damage is skipped whole, its clock and touches kept, so it hurts again as it would have when switched back on; an off hitbox hits nothing, armed or not, its activation and window kept.
- **A lethal kill:** `kill(world, target)` applies a request with a fourth `HitFlags` bit, `LETHAL` (A15): it skips conversion, penetration, resistances and immunity, empties the shield and takes all the health as one untyped part that passes the window, so a kill always lands on the living; the dead and the despawning take no request (A15), so a kill never runs twice; `Damaged` and the `killed` cues follow as for any kill, and its explain record names it lethal. `DamageRequest` stays 152 bytes.
- **The death policy** (S-142): `health`'s `disarm` (false by default, skipped when false, so 02's `saves` bytes hold; A16): at a kill, where the pipeline hurts the health (A15), the entity's contact damage and hitbox are switched off and its `PlatformerController` stops its run, keeps its vertical speed and locks its input for good, as 01's dead player is (A17). A heal never revives the dead, so nothing switches back; a game that brings one back turns them on.
- **A blink** (S-159): `lifetime`'s `blink`, `{ "last": 2, "rate": [5, 10] }`, `last` above 0, each rate above 0, `[5, 5]` by default: over its last `last` seconds the entity's `Visibility` is off for the second half of each period, the rate rising linearly from the first to the second, by the phase `r0·τ + (r1 − r0)·τ²/(2·last)` at `τ` seconds into the blink, so a blink is a function of the elapsed time. The tick lists blinkers beside its finished entities and writes their `Visibility` after its query, inserting one at the first hide, so its query is not widened (A3, A18). Saved with the lifetime, skipped when none.
