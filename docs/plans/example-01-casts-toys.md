### 2.2 Tests are the spec

- Each step keeps all 466 of 01's run tests passing with their asserted outcomes. A test's body changes only where it reads, spawns or calls what the step removes, ported to its equivalent, each port counted in the evidence row.
- An asserted value moves only where the step's Moves names it, by at most one fixed step in time, 1 % in an amount or 1 px in a place, its old and new value listed; any other changed outcome stops the step (§6). No test is deleted but one whose subject the step deletes.
- New tests only where a done-when names them. The kit's fields take kit tests; they add no genre claim, so no recipe.

### 2.3 The ready event (Step 1)

- `AbilityReady { entity, name }`, a plain event `AbilitiesPlugin` registers beside `AbilityUsed` and `Conducted`, sent at each edge `ability_ready` marks (A4), whether or not a cue names it (Q3): in the tick, as an entry's cooldown ends with a charge left or a charge comes back from none, before that step's uses. Its `name` is the entry's, a `String` as `AbilityUsed`'s (Q4). It is no cue event, so no catalogue line (A18). Re-exported beside `AbilityUsed`.

### 2.4 The HUD's flashes and fade (Step 2)

- `spells_hud` reads every `AbilityReady` through a cursor it keeps, each frame, the HUD's absence included. For the run's player and a slot whose spell's ability it names, a cooldown of at least `FLASH_COOLDOWN` and still ready at the read (`Abilities::ready`), it starts the slot's white flash: `UiTrack::background(faded(WHITE, 120), faded(WHITE, 0), FLASH_SECS)` on the game clock, on the slot's flash panel. The `ready` read keeps today's outcome where a cast restarts the cooldown in its ready step: no flash.
- `refused` starts the selected slot's red flash the same way from `faded(danger, 110)`; `summon_turned` starts the name plate's chain, its opacity 1 for 0.9 s, then 1 to 0 over `NAMED_FADE`, on the game clock.
- `Hud` loses `ready`, `refused`, `named` and `cooling`, and `alpha` goes; the update writes `Hud` back only when its scale changes, so the per-frame box goes (A2).
- What moves: a ready flash during a red one replaces it (one track a property, A5), where the red showed out its 0.25 s and the white its rest (Q5); a flash's alpha rounds by `lerp_u8`, as `alpha` rounded; neither asserted (A17).

### 2.5 The HUD's setters and pips (Step 3)

- The ten setter guards go: each value goes straight to `set_plain_text`, `set_image` or `set_layout_style`, which mark nothing on an equal value, and `text_of` goes. The panel's filter stays, comparing the tree's single span with the data's text without allocating, so it still copies only a changed text.
- A slot whose ability has `charges` takes pips, one a charge of its max: `Slot::pips` becomes the pip row's `Option<WidgetId>`, its pips the row's children, read by index, lit up to the charges left. `PIPS` and the `Spell::IronBlock` branch go. Only the iron block has charges, so the bar shows its same three pips.

### 2.6 Clip sides by the aim, the harness's sprite names (Step 4)

A one-shot of an aim-sided set keeps the side its first frame showed (Q8); every other set picks as today. `CLIPS_FORM` and the describe snapshot's clips lines say so; the form is one described type, so no shape and no numeric field joins the census.
- Umbrella: `Harness::load_manifest` interns every manifest sprite name, sheet frames included, sorted, as a window's load does, before its other units, and still registers no sprite: no size, no texture (Q6). `load_manifest_still_registers_no_sprite` becomes `load_manifest_interns_its_sprite_names_and_registers_no_sprite`: the hero's and the sheet's frames' names interned, none with a registered sprite. Known issues' P3 row and the clips-facing follow-up's first clause go; the `MARK_SPRITES` line goes with it at Step 5.

### 2.7 The spells' icons and gestures (Step 5)

- What moves: a re-cast within a gesture's 0.3 s restarts its clip, where 01's continued it (Q7); a one-shot counts its 300 ms from the frame after its play, within a frame of 01's; neither asserted (A17).

### 2.8 Aim poses from a strip (Step 6)
