### 2.4 The geyser's phases and touch (Step 2)

The runtime and test ports from `W/7.13e-step6-geyser-machine/tracked.patch` (it applies to this tree), then the migration its `NOTES.md` designs.

- **The machine.** `GeyserPhase` gains `Choked`. `geyser::machine()` builds `StateMachinePlugin::<GeyserPhase>` with `after(Idle, IDLE_SECS, Sputter)`, `after(Sputter, SPUTTER_SECS, Erupt)`, `after(Erupt, ERUPT_SECS, Idle)` and `after(Choked, CRUST_SECS, Idle)`; the level plugin registers `StateMachine<GeyserPhase>` as the component and the saved component `geyser_phase`, as `enemy_state` is (A7).
- **Hooks.** Entering `Erupt` switches the touch on; leaving it, or entering `Choked`, switches it off. An enter hook plays its look only when the machine has a `previous` state, so a cycle starting part-way starts quietly: `Sputter` the gurgle, `Erupt` the key burst and the roar, `Choked` the steam burst and its sound, through `audio::play` and `spawn_burst` as today.
- **The start.** `start` gives each unstarted geyser `StateMachine::new(phase).starting_at(elapsed)` from its offset (`at_offset` returning the phase and the seconds into it) and its touch on only in an eruption; the touch stays on the entity. `set(world, geyser, phase, elapsed)` is the tests' staging.
- **The chill** stays in `geyser_tick`: sprayed, the cold builds and at `CRUST_COLD` it calls `go(Choked)`, the cold reset, entered in the same step (A7); a choked geyser holds as it is. ### 2.6 The kit's lift: riders by a filter, a ride back (Step 4)

Both are fields of a path's `rider` rule, none of them a new item; a lift without them behaves and saves as it does (A13).

- **`filter`**, a `TagFilter`, none by default (`with_rider_filter(filter)` in code, which panics without a rider rule): with one, a controller marks the lift only when `TagFilter::matches` passes its `Tags`, read as every kit filter reads them, so one without `Tags` passes a filter with no `all` and no `any` (`K/foundation/tags.rs:226-229`). What the filter refuses still rides by the carry and neither boards the lift nor holds it at its far end. The controller reads the rule, then marks (`K/motion/platformer.rs:1969-1976`), cloning nothing.
- **`two_way`**, false by default (`two_way()` in code, on a lift): at either end the lift leaves `board` seconds after a rider steps onto it afresh, one who was not on it when it arrived or who has stepped off since (a step with no mark); a rider it carried there holds it where it stands until it steps off; at its last point it still comes back `back` seconds after its last rider left, the count starting over each time a rider leaves. A one-way lift keeps today's rule at both ends.

### 2.7 The kit's follower paused, turned back and read (Step 5)

- **`set_paused(bool)`, `is_paused()`:** a paused follower's step does nothing along its path: no time, no hold seconds, its rider's mark left unread for the step it resumes at, no cue. ### 2.10 The pit's protocol

01's pit pin is exact (A11), and Steps 2, 3 and 6 change systems, orders or archetypes inside its window. Each of them does this in place of the plain guard check:

1. With the step built and every other test passing, `cargo test --test game -- guards::` passes, and nothing more is owed, or fails on `allocations`, `allocated_bytes` or `archetype_moves` alone.
2. The probe (`W/pit-probe/pit_probe.sh`: the guard's size histogram for `base` and for `step`; then per-size backtraces for each size whose count differs; `diff.py` over the two) attributes every differing bucket to a site. `base` is HEAD's copy of every file the step changed that the run loads, swapped in through `BASE_FILES`: its `src/` and `tests/` files, and at Step 6 the lift's prefab and the rules' `assets/` copy too (with the manifest, if it moved), since HEAD's `ElevatorDef` cannot read the new prefab. `crates/tungsten/src/testing/guard.rs`, which the probe patches, is restored from HEAD and compared (`cmp`) after each run.
3. The move is accepted when every bucket's site is in the step's allowance below and each pinned line stays within its bound; the evidence row quotes the old and new lines and the decomposition (size, count, site, cause).
4. Then `TUNGSTEN_GUARDS_WRITE=1 cargo test --test game -- guards::`, `git diff tests/game/guards/pit.json` showing those lines alone, and `cargo test --test game -- guards::` → exit 0.
