### 2.2 A held trigger, a direction and the refill (Step 2)

### 2.4 Spawns by their centre, their motion kept, a lob (Step 4)

- **A centre:** an entity's centre is its collider's, else its sprite's drawn centre, else its point; a prefab's centre offset is its `rigid_body`'s `offset`, else its sprite's drawn centre from its point, else 0, read from its registry entry at the use (A14, A15). A drawn centre is the default extract's and the looks' (`present/looks.rs:1043-1085`: the anchor, scale, flip and rotation), so a sprite anchored at its centre adds nothing; that geometry becomes one crate helper. A live entity's reads its `Transform` and flip; a prefab's reads its sprite's asset at the transform a spawn gives it, scale 1 and no turn, since a prefab holds no `transform` (core `assets/prefab.rs:60`, `:433`). `centre_of(world, entity)` beside `point_of` is public.
- **Centred spawns:** the spawn payload's `centred` (false by default) puts the prefab's centre on the user's centre plus the offset along the direction; the `prefab` and `roll` cue actions gain object forms, `{ "id", "centred" }` and `{ "table", "centred" }`, beside their ID and entry forms, putting each prefab's centre on the occurrence's position (S-88).
- **Motion kept:** a spawn with a speed sets the prefab's `LinearMotion` velocity and keeps its acceleration, read from its registry entry; no spawned prefab in the template or the fixtures has one, and 01's missile gets the motion its adoption rebuilds before its first step (A17), so this is the default (S-186).
- **The aim's point:** a `nearest` or `random` target's point (its centre when centred), else the user's `Aim`'s: the cursor's world point for `from: cursor`, the target's point for `from: target`, none for `intent` and `movement`. `Aim::point()` reads it, set each aim step beside the direction and not saved (A19).
- **A lob:** the spawn payload's `lob`, `{ "speed": 600, "time": [0.3, 1.2] }`, in place of a `speed`: the velocity that lands the prefab on the aim's point at the end of a flight of `distance / speed` seconds clamped to `time` and rounded to whole fixed steps, for the integration that moves it, by the body kind its registry entry gives. A `linear_motion` moves by the step's mean velocity, exact for the continuous `(to − from) / T − ½ a T` (`motion/linear.rs:275-276`); a dynamic body adds its gravity before it moves each substep `h` (core `physics/step/mod.rs:1528`, `:1612`), so its launch is `(to − from) / T − ½ g (T + h)`, `h` the fixed step over its substeps as the step divides it. The destinations: a dynamic body's `Velocity` under `PhysicsConfig.gravity` times its `gravity_scale`, since `linear_motion` skips a dynamic body (`motion/linear.rs:281-284`; core `physics/step/mod.rs:1544-1551`, `physics/plugin.rs:341`), else its `linear_motion`'s velocity under that motion's acceleration (A18). The load refuses a `lob` beside a `speed` above 0, or on a prefab with neither a dynamic body nor a `linear_motion`; no point refuses the use as `no_target` before the spend. `lob_velocity(from, to, time, gravity)` is public, for 7.10g's carry.
