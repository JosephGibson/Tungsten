### 2.8 The geyser's phases and touch (Step 6)

- **The machine.** `GeyserPhase` gains `Choked`. The level plugin builds `StateMachinePlugin::<GeyserPhase>` with `after(Idle, IDLE_SECS, Sputter)`, `after(Sputter, SPUTTER_SECS, Erupt)`, `after(Erupt, ERUPT_SECS, Idle)` and `after(Choked, CRUST_SECS, Idle)`, and registers `StateMachine<GeyserPhase>` as the component and the saved component `geyser_phase`, as `enemy_state` is (A15).
- **Hooks.** Entering `Erupt` switches the touch on; leaving it, or entering `Choked`, switches it off.
