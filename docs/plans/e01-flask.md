## 2. Design

### 2.1 Poison

- `damage.poison` in `damage_types/game.json`: its label in poison green, sRGB (132, 214, 64), written in the data's linear form (about 0.231, 0.672, 0.051) as the others are, and `School::Poison` holding the sRGB form; one rung, `{"effect": "poisoned", "needs": 0.1, "window": 3, "requires": ["living"], "blocked_by": ["status.frozen"]}`, so a new splash refreshes a running poison. At 0.1 the troll needs 4 poison, the beetle 3 and a goblin 1.5, so any splash poisons what it reaches.
- `poisoned`: `{"duration": 6, "period": 0.5, "damage": {"damage.poison": 3}, "grant": ["status.poisoned"]}`, 36 over its run. `troll_regen` and `dummy_regen` gain `status.poisoned` in `suppressed_by` (A12).
- `living` on the bat, crawler, goblin, troll, beetle and dummy prefabs (Q68): not the wisp, not the wizard. `Kind::Poison`, `POISONED`, `LIVING` in `combat/mod.rs`.
- The look: a green tint after burning in `tint`'s order, so a shocked, poisoned target shows violet on the crackle's even frames and green on the odd ones; `StatusLook::Poisoned` with `ex10_poison_bubbles`; an overlay `ex10_status_poison_<class>_<0-3>` at 8 Hz; `Sound::Poisoned` once as poisoned applies (`EffectApplied`), at the target.

### 2.2 The flask and the eleventh slot

- **Slots (Q43).** `Spell::ToxicFlask` (ability `toxic_flask`, icon `ex10_icon_toxic_flask`, `Gesture::Toss`, pressed). `SLOTS` and `SLOT_ACTIONS` at 11: the flask fifth (index 4), then the force push, the hole, iron, the bowling ball, the small balls and the summon; `spell_minus` on Minus selects the eleventh. `input.json`: `zoom_in` on Wheel Up, `zoom_out` on Wheel Down, `spell_next` and `spell_prev` on RB and LB alone (Q1); `BINDABLE_ACTIONS` gains `("spell_minus", "Spell -")` (Q12).
- **The HUD.** Its arrays take `SLOTS.len()` (a `SLOT_COUNT` const), the name plate the summon's slot found from `SLOTS`, the bar 11 tiles.
- **Tests choose by spell.** `spells::slot_of(spell) -> Option<usize>`, and every `choose` call in `tests/game` names its spell, so a later slot change moves no test (A3).
- **The cast.** `press` refuses with "no room" at no cursor or `FLASK_CAP` 3 in flight, spending nothing; else the ability (12 mana scaled by `spell_cost`, 0.6 s cooldown, aim target, `casting`), then `toys::flask::throw(world, player, centre, cursor)` and `Sound::FlaskThrown`.
- **The flight (Q10).** The flask is the fire missile's machinery with the world's gravity (A7): the `toxic_flask` prefab holds 01's `toxic_flask` marker (`ToxicFlask`, registered as `fire_missile` is), a kit `projectile` (range 3,000 px, no bounces or passes, so any wall ends it), `linear_motion` with acceleration (0, `GRAVITY`) and its 16 px sprite centred at (8, 8), z 14, its 4-frame loop; no body. `throw` spawns it at once, its sprite's centre on the wizard's (its `Position` and `PrevPosition` at that corner, which `flask_contacts` reads), with the run's next save ID and his `Source`, its velocity `troll::ballistic(centre, cursor, t, gravity)` with `t = clamp(sqrt(2 d / g), 0.25, 0.9)` s, `d` the distance, `g` 3,600: on the level its apex rises about a quarter of the distance. `LinearMotion` is exact for a constant acceleration, so it passes through the cursor; with no body it passes through the wizard and nothing pushes it.
- **The burst.** As the missile's: `flask_contacts`, in the overlap phase before the kit's projectile step, bursts each flask in save-ID order at the first point of its centre's step segment where `first_contact`, given the flask's radius 5 in place of `MISSILE_RADIUS`, finds a tile or a non-sensor body but the wizard's; `flask_ends`, after the projectile step, bursts one the kit ended at a wall, swept over its step so an oblique landing ends it on that step, or at its range, in the air. Nothing rests, so nothing waits. The burst plays `ex10_toxic_flask_burst` once (96 px, centred), `ex10_flask_splash` particles and `Sound::FlaskBurst`, and the flask goes.
- **The splash (Q8, Q9).** Each live target with health tagged `living`, never the wizard, whose surface lies within `SPLASH_RADIUS` 96 of the burst takes `SPLASH_POISON` 16 at its centre falling to half at the edge, as `Hit::new(target, [(Kind::Poison, amount)]).at(burst).spell()`, `.from(player)` while he lives, in save-ID order: credited to the wizard, doubled under arcane power with its cost (A8). The direct hit feeds the poison ladder.

### 2.3 Armour and Corrosive

- **Armour (Q3, Q6).** An `armoured` tag, declared, on the beetle's prefab now and the armoured troll's in Step 5. `enemies::armour::{armoured, front, rear}` take `beetle::front`'s and `rear`'s place by tag; the gun sweeps live armoured hurtboxes; `shell` turns a shot off a live front that is neither frozen nor corroded; the bullet's filter becomes `none: ["armoured"]`; `Hit::request`'s rear penetration reads `armour::rear`. Iron's crush keeps `is_beetle`. The beetle's table stays (its test pins it); the armoured troll's is armour's: physical 0.25 and fire, cold, shock and poison 0.75.
- **Corroded (Q4, Q69).** `corroded`: no duration, `grant: ["status.corroded"]`, and `override: 1` on the resists of `damage.physical`, `damage.fire`, `damage.cold`, `damage.shock` and `damage.poison`. The overrides sit on the types, never on `damage`, so shocked's ×1.5 there holds (A13); a corroded beetle loses its shock ×2 with its shell. It lasts until death and is saved with the enemy's effects.
- **Corrosive (Q5).** At the end of `combat_reactions`, each live `armoured` target carrying `status.poisoned` and `status.shocked` and not `status.corroded`, in save-ID order, takes `corroded` at once (`EffectRequest::apply`, no source), with `ex10_corrode_burst` and `Sound::Corroded` at its centre. It reads the step's final tags, so either order corrodes, and a target that loses one status before the other lands is not corroded.
- **The look (Q11).** A dull verdigris tint under every other status's, and `StatusLook::Corroded` with sparse `ex10_corrode_fizz`; no overlay.

### 2.4 The armoured troll

- `Variant { Plain, Armoured }` (snake case, `Plain` the default), `Enemy::variant` with `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = …)]`, so an older save reads plain. The prefab, icon and clip stem come by kind and variant: `troll_armoured`, `ex10_icon_troll_armoured`, `troll_armoured_*` (A17: `clip` reads a lift and the walk by the stem-free part); the body, `KindInfo`, the ice cube and the frost stay the troll's.
- `troll_armoured.json` is `troll.json` with `variant: "armoured"`, the `armoured` tag, armour's table and its own sprite and animation.
- `spawn` and `summon` keep their signatures for the plain variant and call new `spawn_as` and `summon_as`, which take it, so the 18 test calls of the two in 10 files stand; `Summoned::Variant(EnemyKind, Variant)` joins `Summoned` for the armoured troll, so `Summoned::Enemy`'s matches stand, the summon-order test's among them (`tests/game/enemies.rs:2984-2990`); `SUMMONS` appends `armoured troll` tenth, after the keg, so saved indices hold; the save's respawn calls `spawn_as` with `saved.enemy.variant`.
- The bridge troll becomes the armoured one (Q2): `level.json`'s `enemies[12]` takes `kind: "troll_armoured"`, so the Tiled class names the prefab and the map's 29 enemies and their save IDs stand.

### 2.5 Saves: schemas 20 and 21

A save names its schema, and a load at the same schema skips `migrate` (`crates/tungsten-core/src/save.rs:290-291`), so every commit that changes what a save means bumps it; a save one of this plan's intermediate commits writes then migrates once.

- **Schema 20, with the eleventh slot in Step 3.** `to_20`, chained after `to_19` in every arm, moves a saved `spell` of 4 or more up one and gives the player's `abilities` `toxic_flask`'s entry, ready, where it is absent (a duplicate fails the load, `crates/tungsten-kit/src/intent/ability.rs:1029-1035`), as schema 8 added the staff light's (`src/save.rs:315-326`).
