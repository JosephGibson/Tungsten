## 2. Design

The shapes below are this plan's; the decision entry (step 15) records them.

### 2.1 Rows

This plan lands P7-10 (tile layers among sprites), P7-13 (pan, ducks, looping cue voices), P7-14 (text outline), P7-15 (sheets), K2 (particle depth), K4 (animation rate), K9 (lit flash), S-38 (K21, emitters at run time), S-25 (light tweens), S-27 (particle clips, stretch, stops), S-29 (attractors), S-40 (backdrops), S-46 (flips), S-58 (variants), S-63 (music per state), S-70 (draw helpers), S-71 (fragment-only materials), S-76 (beams), S-87 (one-shot ends), S-89 (start colours), S-95 (flat quads, segments), S-97 (tinted and unlit layers and sprites), S-113 (frame sounds), S-117 (stock glow), S-137 (anchors), S-138 (particles leave with their state), S-143 (extract slots), S-148 (headless sprites), S-173 (plays by ID) and S-179 (the manifest from `Config`). A row lands with its engine or kit part; its 01 sites move at 7.13 and its template sites at 7.11, except S-63, S-173 and S-179, whose template sites move here.

### 2.2 The startup line (T2)

- `StartupTimings`, a resource the app inserts once loaded. Phase durations, in milliseconds: `window`, `renderer`, `boot` (the boot group), `load` (the rest of the units), within it `sprites` and `sounds` (their decoding) and `pack` (packing and uploads), then `startup` (the hook) and `audio` (its start). Milestones, in milliseconds from `run_window`'s start: `first_frame` (the first frame presented, boot or not) and `loaded` (the moment frame 1 may start: the load, the hook and audio done), the same endpoint in a synchronous load and a sliced one. And `boot_frames` and `slice_max` (§2.6).
- With `TUNGSTEN_PERF_LOG`, one line once loaded: `startup: window=… renderer=… … boot_frames=… slice_max=…`; `perf_stats.py` reads each field as one sample of `startup.<field>` per run.
- A `startup` script probe returns the resource as JSON (A30).
- **The capture** comes before any loading change: three release runs each of 01 and the template, ended at frame 2 (`TUNGSTEN_SMOKE_FRAMES=2`), their medians, the `loaded` milestone and the decode share (the durations `sprites` plus `sounds` over `load`) in the run's folder, functional only (`D-226`). It decides Q2 and is step 3's baseline.

### 2.3 The manifest from `Config` (S-179)

`tungsten.json`'s `assets` section: `manifest` (a path; absent, none) and `hot_reload` (a bool; unset, on in debug builds), unknown keys failing as the other sections' do. When the config names a manifest, `App::new` makes it the root, failing when its file is missing, and with hot reload on watches its folder and the manifest, as `enable_hot_reload` does. A config that names none sets no root, as today: 02's `particles` fills its registries in code before `run` and sets no root, and a manifest load would replace them (A36). `set_manifest_roots` and `enable_hot_reload` replace both. The template's `tungsten.json` names `assets/manifest.json`, which `tungsten new` copies, and its `main` drops its two calls; 01 keeps its calls until 7.13.

### 2.4 Sprite sheets and anchors (P7-15, S-137)

- **Sheets:** a manifest section `sheets`: `"troll_walk": { "path": "sprites/troll_walk.png", "frame": [96, 96], "count": 8, "filter": "nearest", "normal_map": …, "emissive_mask": …, "anchor": [48, 65] }`. Its frames are the image's `frame`-sized cells, left to right, then top to bottom, `count` of them (default every whole cell); each registers as sprite `<sheet>_<n>` from 0, with the sheet's filter, its siblings cropped alike and its anchor. A frame ID another entry takes fails the load (`D-017`); so do a `count` past the cells and a `frame` larger than the image. Animations name frames as they name sprites, so a per-frame family moved into a sheet of the same names keeps its clips (Q8).
- **Loading:** a sheet decodes once and each frame becomes the packer's input as its own image would be, so the atlas and the UVs are what one file per frame gives. `SpriteAsset` gains `rect` (the frame's place in its file, `None` for a whole file). The registry maps a path to all its sprites; a sheet edit decodes the file once and writes each frame in place, or repacks the filter when one grew; a repack decodes each file once. `tungsten check` decodes each sheet and checks its cells and siblings.
- **Anchors:** `anchor: [x, y]` in source pixels on a sprite or a sheet; `SpriteAsset::anchor`. An anchored sprite draws with that point at the entity's position, scaled with it, and turns about it: the extract moves the quad by `R(c − a) − (c − a)` (`c` the quad's centre, `a` the anchor), since the shader turns about the centre, so neither the shader nor the instance changes. A sprite without one draws from its top-left and turns about its centre, as today (Q9).

### 2.5 Sprites in the headless harness (S-148)

`Harness::register_sprites(path)` registers each sprite's and sheet frame's metadata from a manifest: its size from the image's header (`image::image_dimensions`), lit when it names a normal map, its anchor and nine-slice, on one spare texture handle per filter with full UVs, through the function `tungsten check` uses. `load_manifest` is unchanged, so every existing harness test and guard draws as before (Q6).

### 2.6 Boot loading (§5.4)

- **Units:** `asset_loader::LoadPlan` lists a merged manifest's load as units in `load_all`'s order: each sprite or sheet decoded, each filter's pack and upload, each animation file, each font, the themes, the shaders, the materials, each sound decoded, each tilemap, the particle meshes, the particles, the physics names, the prefabs and data, the cross-checks. `load_all` runs them all at once, so `tungsten check`, `describe` and the harness keep today's load; a unit's error fails the run as today. Its progress is units done over units.
- **The boot group:** a manifest section `"boot": { "logo": "<sprite>", "bar": "<sprite>", "font": "<font>" }`, each optional, checked on the merged graph (`D-089`); two roots setting one fail the load. `scripts/check-repo.py` reads the section as IDs (A31).
- **Boot frames:** `resumed` merges the manifests, builds the plan, interns every sprite name, sheet frames included, in sorted order (A3), loads the group's sprites onto pages of their own, under texture handles the renderer mints down from `u32::MAX`, a range its counter never reaches (A34), and its font, and asks for a redraw. The first redraw draws the boot view and presents before any unit runs; each later one while units remain runs them until 12 ms have passed (a unit that starts finishes), draws and presents the view when 33 ms have passed since the last, and asks for the next redraw. When the last unit ends: the boot pages drop (the full pack holds the group's sprites and mints its handles from the counter as a synchronous load does, so every handle and UV is that load's), the startup hook runs, audio starts, `StartupTimings` is written, the frame clock resets as `resumed` resets it today, and the next redraw is frame 1, with `Stage::Startup`.
- **While booting:** no stage runs and no boot frame counts for scripts, smoke, captures or telemetry; resizes apply; key, pointer and pad input is dropped and cleared at the end; a close request ends the run at once, with no last frame, since nothing is loaded to save (`D-180`'s last frame starts with frame 1); hot reload paths wait in the watcher's queue.
- **The view:** by default, the clear colour; with a group, the logo centred at its size × the window's scale, the bar 16 units below it stretched to the progress, and with a font, `NN %` under it. `App::set_boot_view(f)` replaces it, `f(&World, BootProgress)` returning the frame's sprite batches and text sections in window pixels (7.11's splash).
- No worker thread (Q2). Every game boots this way, a game without a group on a plain frame (Q3).

### 2.7 Sound hot reload

`SoundRegistry` keeps each sound's path (`id_for_path`) and replaces a sound's data under its handle. An edited sound file (the extensions `SoundData::decode` takes) decodes again; a manifest edit adds new sounds and refreshes the volume, loop, bus and voice limit of the rest, and a removed sound stays, warned, as a sprite does. Then the audio restarts as it does after a lost device: the stream drops and reopens with the registry's sounds, the latch replaying the master, the buses, their ducks (§2.8) and the loops from their start; playing one-shots end (Q5). The latch is refreshed first, since a reopen keeps the first `init`'s voice limits by handle (A6): the outbox takes the new table's limits, drops the latched loops of a sound that now plays nothing and keeps each sound's newest loops within its new limit, as the new mixer keeps them when the replay starts them in order, so a sound the reload added latches like any other. `D-053`'s session-static clause and DESIGN's reload matrix change (step 15).

### 2.8 Pan, ducks and plays by ID (P7-13's engine part, S-173)

- **Pan:** each voice has a pan, −1 (left) to 1 (right), 0 by default: `AudioCommand::Play` carries it, `AudioCommands::play_panned` sets it and `set_voice_pan(voice, pan, seconds)` ramps it. The mixer takes left × min(1, 1 − pan) and right × min(1, 1 + pan), so a centred voice mixes as today, sample for sample (Q12).
- **Ducks:** `AudioCommands::duck_bus(bus, gain, seconds)` ramps a bus's duck, 1 by default, which multiplies the player's volume and leaves it as set; the latch replays a duck under 1 on a resync or restart (A6).
- **Plays by ID:** `tungsten::core::audio::play(world, id, fade)` plays a manifest sound at its volume and loop, faded in, and returns its voice, `None` for an unknown ID. The shell's `play` calls it (A10).
- The latch keeps a loop's pan as it keeps its volume, and the latch-against-mixer test's patterns take pan and duck commands; the audio ring rules stand (`D-179`).

### 2.9 Loops that follow entities, and music per state (P7-13's kit part, S-63)

- **`SoundLoop`** (kit, scene `sound_loop`: `{ "id": …, "fade": 0.2 }`): plays a manifest sound looping while its entity lives with the component, at its volume × the `CueListener`'s gain at the entity's position, panned by the listener, both followed each frame by ramps (a change under 0.01 sends nothing); it stops over its fade when the entity despawns or drops the component. At most `SoundLoops::max_voices` loops (a kit resource, 16 by default) hold voices at once, the nearest first across every sound, each sound within its own `max_voices`; a loop past the listener's hearing or outside that ranking holds none, and one that comes back starts over its fade (Q13). With the music's tracks, one for each state on the stack that has one, that leaves one-shots most of the mixer's 64 slots, and the mixer drops a play only when every slot holds a playing loop (A7), so no loop's play is lost unseen on the one-way ring; a game's own loops besides these are the game's to count. Without a listener, every loop plays at its volume, centred.
- **The listener's pan:** `CueListener::with_pan(width)`: pan = (x − listener x) / width, clamped; a loop and a one-shot `sound` cue take it. A listener without it pans nothing, so today's cue sounds stay centred.
- **Music per state:** `StateMusic` (umbrella, beside `StateStack`): `set(state_id, sound, fade)`, `clear(state_id)`. Its system, after the dispatcher, plays the track of the highest state on the stack that has one at its manifest volume and loop; a covered state's track falls to 0 over its fade, keeping its voice, and comes back when its state is again the highest with a track; a state that leaves the stack stops its voice over its fade. A state without a track leaves the one below playing (Q14).
- **The template:** the shell sets the title's and game over's tracks once in its plugin, the sample its gameplay's and pause's; `duck`, `Music` and the screens' own play and stop go. 01's shell copy follows (`D-197`); 01's own run and pause music stay its code until 7.13. The title's and game over's commands keep their frames and order: the system runs in the frame the state enters, after its `on_enter`.

### 2.10 Animation rates, one-shot ends and frame sounds (K4, S-87, S-113)

- **Rate:** `AnimationState::speed` (1; a value not finite or negative reads 1); the kit's tick advances by dt × speed; the `animation` component takes `"speed"`; `CharacterAnimator` carries an entity's speed across its clip changes.
- **One-shot ends:** `AnimationState::despawn_at_end` (`"despawn": true`): when a one-shot finishes, the tick ends its entity through the kit's despawn (`end(world, entity, "animation")`); a looping clip never does.
- **Frame sounds:** an animation file's frame takes `"sound": "<sound id>"`, checked against the sounds at load and reload. The tick plays it when the clip enters that frame, at its manifest volume × the listener's gain and pan at the entity's position, as a `sound` cue does; when one tick enters several frames, only the last that has a sound plays (Q15). `AnimationFrame` gains `sound`; its literals take `None`.

### 2.11 Particles (K2, S-38, S-27, S-29, S-89, S-138)

- **Config fields**, each defaulting to today's particle: `z_order` (K2; the newborn's `Sprite.z_order` from its spawn, so it never draws at 0 first); `clip` (an animation ID: the particle shows the clip's frame for its age, looping as the clip does, a one-shot holding its last); `stretch` (seconds: the quad stretches along its velocity by |v| × stretch and turns with it, its angular velocity ignored); `stop` (`{ "mask": [<layers>] }`, by number or by name, names checked with the cross-checks once the physics names load: at spawn a ray along the first velocity, `cast_ray` over bodies and tiles on those layers, ends the particle's life where it would reach the surface, drag counted and gravity not); `attractor` (`{ "pull": …, "swirl": … }` in px/s², toward the live emitter's position, the swirl perpendicular to the pull); `start_color` (`{ "range": [a, b] }`, one random draw between two colours, or `{ "by_direction": <curve> }` over the first velocity's angle in turns, no draw), multiplied into `tint`. Validation refuses non-finite values, a negative stretch, an unknown clip or layer, and `z_order`, `clip` or `stretch` on a mesh particle (`D-093`). The struct literals take the defaults (A24).
- **Draws and costs:** only `start_color.range` draws, and only when set, so every existing stream stays (A25); the attractor's emitter positions and the stops' casts are gathered only when some live config uses them, into buffers kept across frames, so a frame without them allocates nothing new.
- **Emitters at run time (K21, S-38):** `ParticleEmitter` gains `rate_scale` (1; it scales a continuous rate), `direction` (`None`, or a direction replacing a cone's or vector's) and `paused` (false; nothing emits while set, and a burst not yet fired fires on resume). A changed `config` takes its new snapshot at the emitter's next tick, keeping its random stream; a reload alone still changes no live emitter (`D-050`).
- **Owners (S-138):** a state's exit despawns the live particles whose emitter its exit despawns, so a title's stars leave with the title; particles whose emitter died before age out as today.

### 2.12 Flips, lit flashes, unlit sprites and light tweens (S-46, K9, S-97's sprites, S-25)

- **Flips:** `SpriteFlip(bool)` (scene `sprite_flip`), a component kept on the entity so a turn moves no archetype: a flipped sprite mirrors about its anchor, or its centre without one, by a negative UV width, and the lit shader mirrors the normal map's x where the UV width is negative. The kit's `Facing` takes `"flip": true` to write it from its direction, and a `CharacterAnimator` clip set's `left` becomes optional: without one the right clip shows, flipped (Q10).
- **Unlit:** `Unlit` (scene `unlit`), a marker: a sprite with maps draws on the stock pipeline (S-97's far props).
- **Lit flashes (K9):** a lit sprite keeps its material's uniform block, and the lit shader mixes its lit colour toward `v0.rgb` by `f32s[0] × v0.a`, the stock flash's lanes, bound per batch as material batches bind theirs (`D-101`); without a block the amount is 0 and the pixel is today's. The material's shader stays unused on a lit sprite (`D-061`), so the kit's hit flash flashes lit sprites unchanged (Q11).
- **Light tweens (S-25):** `TweenChannel::LightIntensity { from, to }` (`"kind": "light_intensity"`) writes `Light.intensity`; a look is a prefab with a `lifetime` and that tween.

### 2.13 Tile layers, extract slots and backdrops (P7-10, S-97's tiles, S-143, S-40)

- **Layer properties** (a tile layer's Tiled properties): `z`, an integer z-order among sprites (absent: under every sprite, as today); `unlit` (a bool: a lit tileset draws on the stock pipeline); and Tiled's own `tintcolor`, multiplied into each tile's colour. They live in a list parallel to the layers, as their physics does, so `TilemapLayer` keeps its shape (A17).
- **The default base:** the layers without `z` first at the far plane, as today; then the `Sprite` entities, each `z` layer's batches after every sprite of a lower `z_order` and before the rest; under `gpu_depth` each `z` layer takes the depth of its place, the count including the layers. A map without a `z` layer draws as today, instance for instance.
- **Slots (S-143):** `Extracts::add_sprites_under_tiles(f)` draws before the base's bottom tiles, `add_sprites_over_tiles(f)` after them and before the sprites, both at the far plane under `gpu_depth` (later draws win at equal depth, `LessEqual`); `add_sprites` keeps drawing after the base. Under a replaced base both slots draw before it, in that order.
- **Backdrops (S-40):** `Backdrop` (scene `backdrop`): a sprite, a tint, a parallax `factor`, `repeat` (`"x"`, `"y"`, `"xy"`), `fill` (stretched to the view), `drift` (px/s on the game clock) and `foot` (the last texel row stretched down to the view's bottom), with the entity's position its origin. The base draws backdrops first, by `z_order`, then entity, before the under-tiles slot, repeated across the view box.

### 2.14 Materials and draw helpers (S-71, S-117, S-95, S-76, S-70)

- **Fragment-only materials (S-71):** a material module without `vs_main` compiles after a prelude (`render/shaders/material_prelude.wgsl`, compiled in): the camera, texture and sampler bindings, the `Material` block, the instance layout and a `vs_main` passing the uv, the colour and the quad-local position; a module with its own `vs_main` compiles as today. The shader coverage test validates the prelude, and each fragment-only module after it.
- **A white texel and flat quads (S-95):** the renderer keeps a 1 × 1 white texture, registered as the reserved sprite `engine_white`, interned after the manifest's names so their IDs stay (A3). `SpriteInstance::flat(position, size, rotation, color, z_norm)` and `segment(from, to, width, color, z_norm)` (a turned flat quad a width longer, centred on the midpoint) build its instances; an entity draws one as a `Sprite` on `engine_white` scaled by its `Transform`.
- **A stock glow (S-117):** the reserved material `engine_glow`, fragment-only (`stock/glow.wgsl`, compiled in beside the stock post shaders and mirrored in `assets/shaders/stock/`, which the root manifest lists, `D-057`): `v0` its colour, `f32s` its falloff, core boost, dither and peak alpha; drawn on an `engine_white` sprite of the glow's size. A manifest entry under `engine_glow` fails the load, as `engine_mono` is reserved; no manifest in the tree names one.
- **Helpers (S-70, S-76):** in `sprite_extract.rs`, public: `sprite_instance(asset, position, size, rotation, color, z_norm)` from an asset's UVs, `Batches` (pushes an instance into the last batch when drawn alike, else opens one), `view_box(world)` (the world box the default extract culls by), and `beam(batches, asset, from, to, width, offset, color, z_norm)`, which lays the sprite along a segment, the last tile cut short by its UV, `offset` scrolling it.

### 2.15 Text outlines and shadows (P7-14)

- `TextOverrides` gains `outline` (`{ color, width }`) and `shadow` (`{ color, offset }`), inherited as the others; the theme file's `text` object takes them (`"outline": { "color": "#000000c0", "width": 1.5 }`); `WorldLabel` and `TextSection` take both.
- An outlined area draws first as eight copies at its width around it in the outline's colour, a shadow as one copy at its offset under them, then the text; copies share the shaped buffer, except a node with coloured spans, which shapes a second buffer without their colours, kept only while it is outlined or shadowed (A21, Q16). A text without either draws as today.
- **Bounds:** an outlined or shadowed text's paint box grows by the outline's width on every side and by the shadow's offset on its sides, and that box is both its clip bounds, still within its ancestors' clip, and the box the paint list's text batches test for overlaps (A35); its layout size stays, so no layout moves. A world label's cull margin grows the same way.

### 2.16 Prefab sprite variants (S-58)

A prefab's or scene's `sprite` takes `"variants": ["crate_b", "crate_c"]`; the pool is the sprite and its variants, each ID checked at load. An entity spawned from it shows one, picked by its save identity when it has one (`D-230`; the ID's FNV hash modulo the pool) and else by its spawn position's whole pixels; `save::name`, the flush's naming and the restore, which gives a spawn back its saved ID, pick again for an entity holding `SpriteVariants`, so a named or saved spawn shows the same variant after a load with nothing saved (Q17).

A `guards_boot` counter guard runs the plan's units at `min` without a renderer (`LoadPlan::run_headless`, which skips packing, uploads, fonts and shaders) and pins its counts. No budget (9.1).

`examples/03_fixtures`' scene `seams` (`TUNGSTEN_FIXTURE=seams`), from the repository root as its other scenes: assets of its own under `assets/seams/`, listed in its manifest, and pieces each drawing step adds: a lit sprite and its flipped twin under a light at its left, a flashing lit sprite, an unlit one (step 9); a tile layer at a `z` between two sprites, a tinted unlit layer and a repeating backdrop (step 10); a glow, a flat quad, a segment and a beam (step 11); an outlined and a shadowed label (step 12). `tests/integration/seams_regression.rs`, behind `TUNGSTEN_VISUAL_REGRESSION=1` as 03's suites, captures frame 5 and checks each piece by pixels (a mean or a single pixel against a bound, never a golden), and `just visual` runs it.
