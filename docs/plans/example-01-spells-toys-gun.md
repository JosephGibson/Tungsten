### 2.3 The kit's conduction from code (Step 1)

- `conduct_from(world, user, first, &conduct, chained) -> bool` starts a chain at `first` unstruck, `chained` (with `first`) its receivers already struck, its damage and jumps the conduction's; its first jump comes at the next step's `ABILITY_TICK`, as a payload's. It refuses, pushing nothing, a `first` gone, despawning or dead, immune to the tags or passing the stop, a conduction of 0 jumps, and a 65th chain (traced as the payload's).
- Each strike is a blast's hit: a request only on a receiver with live health (a jump still reaches iron, a scrap or a keg); its parts the conduction's damage of its tags; its `context` tags (`with_context`, data `context`); its named preparation (`with_prepare`, data `prepare`, refused while no preparation holds the name, as the blast's); its crit rolled after the preparation from the user's stats; at the receiver's centre. The payload's strike on its target takes the same rules.
- `Conducted { user, receiver, from, to, jump, damage, struck }`, sent at each jump to a receiver: `from` the point it left, `to` the receiver's centre, `jump` from 1, `struck` false where an immune or stopping receiver ends the chain unstruck. A plain event `AbilitiesPlugin` registers, never a cue event, so no catalogue line (A24).
- `stop_struck` (data, `with_stop_struck`): an immune or stopping receiver takes its strike, then ends the chain. `past_gone` (data, `with_past_gone`): with its last receiver gone or despawning, a chain jumps on from that receiver's centre at its strike (the start's at `conduct_from`), kept on the chain; its user's absence still ends it. Both off by default, so today's payloads conduct as before.
- `conductions(world) -> Vec<Conduction>`: each chain in flight in its order, its user, last receiver, chained receivers, damage and jumps left. `Conduct`, `Conducted`, `Conduction`, `conduct_from` and `conductions` re-exported, `Conduct` in the prelude beside `Cone`.

### 2.4 The chains saved (Step 2)

- `AbilitiesPlugin` registers the chains as a saved resource, `conductions`: each chain's user, last receiver and chained receivers by reference, its kept point, damage, jumps left and its conduction's data form, in their order.
- A load drops a chain whose user did not load; a chained receiver that did not load leaves its list; a last receiver that did not load ends its chain, unless `past_gone`, which jumps on from the kept point; the 64 hold.
- A World that never conducts writes no chain; every capture of a World with `AbilitiesPlugin` lists the name's version (A12).
