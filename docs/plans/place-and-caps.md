## 2. Design

### 2.1 The saved work

The patch and `step5-new/` are the map, not a base: Step 1 copies `lifecycle/cap.rs` and `tests/cap.rs` from `step5-new/` and ports the patch's cap hunks by hand with 2.2's changes; Step 2 ports its `place` hunks with 2.3's. The stopped run's design stands where 2.2 and 2.3 do not change it: the frame-stamped queue, `oldest` refusing when only queued spawns fill the group, the ended-list reader, `culled` sent only when named through the kit's reserved list, `Used::NoRoom` and `Used::Capped`, and the reasons `no_room` and `capped`.

### 2.2 A cap (Step 1)

- **The component.** `cap`, a scene component on a prefab (`Cap`): `{ "group": "salt", "max": 2, "past": "oldest" }`; `group` a dotted name (`[a-z][a-z0-9_]*` segments), `max` at least 1, `past` `oldest` (default) or `refuse` (`Past`, non-exhaustive). Rule data a prefab gives, so `UNSAVED_COMPONENTS` lists it as `RULE` and a restored holder takes it back from its prefab (A7). `CapPlugin` registers it, its inspector rows and its description (claiming `rpg` and `shooter`, the fixtures that name it), builds `SourcePlugin` (idempotent), so a capped World derives and saves `Source` whichever plugins it holds (A7), adds the kit's despawn when the schedule lacks it, inserts `Caps` and joins `KitPlugins::set()` after `LevelPlugin`, with no system.
- **What a group counts.** Its holders are the live entities with a `Cap` of that group that carry an admission stamp (a crate-private component a route's admission inserts with the spawn) or a `Source`, neither `Despawning`, nor on the step's ended list (a crate reader, `ending`, in `lifecycle/ended.rs`), nor leaving it this frame as a `become` replaces it (below); a map-placed holder, spawned with neither, stays aside (A7, Q2). To them it adds the group's admissions queued this frame, which land at its one flush: `Caps`, an unsaved resource, counts them per group and lists the frame's leaving receivers, stamped with `Time::frame()` and dropped by a later frame (A5). Each spawn reads its own prefab's `max` and `past` against that count (Q1). A holder counts until an item ends it, a dead one kept by its `Health` included.
- **The oldest.** Holders without a stamp come first, by the kit's save order (restored from a save, or a game's own spawn from a source), then stamped ones by stamp, a count `Caps` keeps; so age holds when an index is reused and in a World that never saves (A6, Q3).
- **One verdict.** A crate function decides, with no side effect: uncapped; room; the oldest holders to end (`oldest`: as many as bring the group under its max, one unless a child, a game's own spawn or a lowered `max` put it over, Q12); or refused (`refuse` with no room, or `oldest` when the live holders cannot make room because this frame's admissions fill it). A capped spawn whose source is `Despawning`, or on the frame's leaving list, is refused before the verdict, culling and counting nothing: the source's despawn or replacement is queued ahead of the spawn, so core drops the spawn at the flush (A8; core `ecs/world.rs:603-609`, `:624-632`). `admit(world, id, source, prepared)` acts on the verdict: it ends each victim through the kit's despawn (`end(.., "cap")`), pushes its `culled` when named, counts the admission and returns the prepared prefab with its stamp; a refusal comes back as its reason, which the route traces where it traces its other skips, once (`source` is `None` for a sourceless spawn, whose trace goes to the route's own subject). Each route calls it after its prefab prepared, so a spawn whose prefab fails culls and counts nothing (A5). A prefab without a `cap` returns after one registry lookup, before any allocation.
- **The routes.** Every kit spawn by prefab ID (A4): an ability's spawn payload, the `prefab` and `roll` cue actions (a world cue's included), the spawner, chemistry's `spawn` output on an entity and on a tile cell (a refusal one more skipped output), and `become`'s replacement, an output's or a timed revert's, whose source is its receiver (core drops it too when an earlier command despawned the receiver, `ecs/world.rs:624-632`). A replacement is admitted with its receiver leaving the group as it arrives: `replace` asks its verdict with the receiver left out and, once it queues the replacement (admitted or uncapped), lists the receiver in `Caps`' leaving list, so a replacement in its receiver's group takes its place, a later admission that frame neither counts nor culls the receiver nor spawns from it, and no cull can pick a receiver whose replacement would survive it (A4); a refused replacement leaves its receiver as it is, a timed revert spent (Q10). A parent's `spawn_children` is not admitted: a child comes with its parent. A spawn its cap refuses does not count against a spawner's `with_count`: the spawner gets it back (Q4).
- **`culled`.** A kit cue event about the entity a cap ended, at its centre (`centre_of`), as a damage event is at its target's (Q7); pushed only when the victim's `cues` or a loaded rule names it (`D-243`'s rule). `AbilityCues` becomes `KitCues` (its entries `KitCue`, with `KitCue::about` naming no ability, its capacity still 32), so the kit's events go from 21 to 22.
- **The late pass.** The ability ticker, the spawner and chemistry run before the cue system, so their `culled` reaches its main pass; a cue action's capped spawn, or a world cue's, ends its victim after that pass has read the list (A9). The cue system's run gains a pass after the world cues and before the loops: it reads what the step's own actions and world cues pushed, prepares as the main pass does, runs those occurrences through the same batch (live, undespawning subjects; their own cues, then rules) and drains its own despawns; what it pushes waits for the next step (Q8). An empty list returns at once. An `ability` cue action's refusal now cues that step, not the next (Q9).
- **Recipes.** `rpg`: the lantern warden's new manual `wisp`, a spawn of a capped `wisp` (`oldest`, 2) whose `culled` spawns a centred `poof`: a third wisp culls the first, the poof's centre on the first wisp's. `shooter`: a new carrier's spawner of `escort`s (`refuse`, 3, five in all): three live, the next refused while full and kept in its count, two more once two are destroyed, then none. New prefabs and data only, none of the determinism script's (A14), and no component whose claim would change (`counters` and `stats` stay out of `shooter`).

### 2.3 The place payload (Step 2)

- **Data form.** `{ "place": { "prefab": "salt", "range": 240 } }`, `range` (> 0) optional, any distance when left out, left out when serialized. In Rust `Payload::Place(Box<Place>)`, `Place::new(prefab).within(range)` with `prefab()` and `range()`, boxed as `SpawnWith`, `Cone` and `Conduct` are, so `Payload` stays 32 bytes (A3); `the_payload_keeps_its_size` pins it. `Place` is exported at the root and in the prelude.
- **The point.** `aim_point`'s, centred: a `nearest` or `random` target's centre, else the user's `Aim::point`; a `self` or `aim` target reads the `Aim`'s (Q11). The prefab's centre (`prefab_centre`) lands on it: its point is the point less that centre.
- **Refused before anything is spent,** after the cooldown, charge, cost and target checks and beside the lob's: `NoTarget` with no point, or one farther than `range` from the user's centre; `NoRoom` when the prefab's body, centred on the point, meets a solid tile or a body that is not dynamic (static, kinematic, a collider without a body), the user's own aside, in any layer, sensors and one-way tiles left out (core `overlap` under the default filter), as 01's props and iron blocks read; a prefab without a body always has room (A10, Q5); `Capped` when its cap's verdict refuses it: `refuses` runs the verdict for each of the use's capped spawns and places in payload order against a tally of the use's earlier admissions (each group's added count and chosen victims, a chosen victim still counted but not chosen again), so two places into one group, or a spawn and then a place, see each other; a place refused there refuses the use, a spawn refused there is only skipped after the spend. The admissions after the spend run in the same order and never meet less room than their checks did: an earlier spawn whose prefab fails to prepare neither culls nor adds, and nothing else between them adds to a group; the price is a use refused when an earlier spawn's culls would have made the room (Q13). A place whose own prefab fails to prepare after its checks is traced and skipped, as a spawn's is (`D-244`). `ability_refused`'s reasons gain `no_room` (5) and `capped` (6) in the occurrence's three reason bits, and a rule's `reason` takes both (A11).
- **The spawn.** From the user (`spawn_prefab_from`), through `admit`, with the sheet's delivery when the ability has one, traced as a spawn; no `placed` event, which 01 holds as its own.
- **Recipes.** `rpg`: the warden's `salt`, a `place` of `salt` at its `nearest` mark within 300 (the mark's tag declared in a new `tags` entry): set down on an open mark, its centre on the mark's; refused `no_room` on a mark inside a static wall, its cooldown unspent. `shooter`: a sapper's `lay_mine`, a `place` of a sensor `claymore` capped at 3 (`refuse`): three laid, the fourth refused `capped` with nothing spent, and a `cue_rules` rule on `ability_refused` with reason `capped` sounding a blip, counted as the dry-fire recipe counts its clicks.
