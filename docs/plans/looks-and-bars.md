## 2. Design

### 2.1 The presence read and the gate (Steps 1, 2)

- **`World::has_any::<T>()`**, `#[must_use]`: whether any live entity holds a `T`. `Archetypes::holds(TypeId)` walks the archetypes, as every query does (A2), and answers on the first one with rows whose sorted key has the type (A3): no shape joins the kept matches, nothing allocates, and an archetype emptied by despawns or removes reads false. Its doc names the use: a gate before a query whose holders are rare, since a new shape regrows its bits whenever an archetype is added (`D-206`). Pending commands count after their flush, as for a query.
- **The gate:** the tag looks' early part and the bars' read `has_any` first. With no holder and no part kept, each returns before its query; with no holder but parts kept (the last holder despawned, or its `Looks` removed), every part goes without a query; with a holder, the stopped patch's query runs. The late pass reads only kept parts (A6). So a World with no holder, 01, the template and 02's `spread` row among them, runs no new query shape and its counts hold; a World with one runs the two queries the stopped patch ran.
- **The stopped run's other options**, rejected: (a) a presence set by the `looks` and `health_bar` spawns, with a mark for code-built holders: a holder a game inserts from Rust without the mark shows nothing, silently, and the kit's tests and recipes insert from code; (b) core's cache grown only for a matching archetype: every guard's reading drops and each pin moves, which is the query paths' cost, 7.14's C4, whose evidence cell gains this regrowth at the close; (c) the two plugins kept out of `KitPlugins::set()`: a prefab naming `looks` in a game on the set fails its load, against the set's contract (A10).
- **The cost:** one walk per gate per frame, two in 01, over the 385 to 448 archetypes its pit's seven words of bits a shape cover (A1), the walk each of its queries already makes (A2) and no allocation. No timed row's workload changes (`D-226`).
- **At 7.13:** once 01's foes wear looks or a bar, the queries run in 01 and its pit pin moves: 7.13's move, with its cause, unless 7.14's C4 lands first (Q5).

### 2.2 Looks by tag (S-10, Step 2)

- **`looks`**, a scene component: a list of `tag_look` `{ "when": <tag filter>, "look": <look>, "grow": 0.4 }`. While `when` passes the holder's tags, the look's tint, overlay, glow and particles show as a status look's, at a share of 1, or rising linearly from 0 over `grow` seconds of game time shown (paused, it holds); a filter that stops passing takes its parts at the next early pass and starts its time again. A light or a sound is refused (`TagLook::new` panics, the data form errs): a status keeps them on the fixed step with its instance. The load checks each filter's tags and each look's IDs. Unsaved presentation: a load shows the parts again from the restored tags.
- **Parts and mirroring:** each passing entry's parts are plain entities the default extract draws (`D-007`), kept by holder and entry index, gone with a holder that is despawned, despawning or without its `Looks`. An overlay's offset and flip follow the holder's drawn flip and turn, as a status overlay's do (A8), whatever wrote the flip (a facing's `flip` or `mirror`, a character animator's mirrored set or aim poses, the game). A holder drawn unflipped whose `Facing` neither turns nor flips it (no `turn`, `flip` or `mirror`) mirrors them by that facing's side instead, left of the vertical, the entry's last side kept while the facing points straight up or down, as a mirror keeps its own (A19); a turning facing's side is in its turn. A crate-private `Facing` reader says whether a facing turns or flips its entity; the side kept is the pass's state, no component's.
- **Placement:** an overlay's point is its target centre less its frame's drawn-centre offset (`sprite_centre` at the origin, with the overlay's scale, turn and flip), so the frame's drawn centre lands on the target whatever its anchor, and holds there as `scale` grows it; for an unanchored frame that is half its scaled size, today's placement (A20). Status overlays share the helper, so an anchored status overlay, off by its scaled anchor today, lands centred too; no fixture, template or 01 overlay has an anchored frame, so no test's positions move.
- **Tints:** a holder's tag tints multiply in entry order, each toward its colour by its share times its alpha, then with its status tints, once a frame over the colour the frame's writers left; the early pass puts the colour back, as for a status (A6).
- **`look_overlay`'s `scale`**, `[from, to]`, `[1, 1]` by default and skipped when default, so saved and data bytes hold: the overlay's scale by its share, a status overlay's by its intensity, a tag look's by its growth.
- **API:** `Looks` (`new`, `with`, `entries`), `TagLook` (`new`, `with_grow`, `grow`), `Overlay::with_scale`, `LooksPlugin`.

### 2.3 The health bar (S-52, Step 2)

- **`health_bar`**, a scene component: `{ "size": [64, 6], "rise": 6, "back": [0.05, 0.04, 0.05, 0.86], "fill": [0.77, 0.19, 0.16, 1.0], "inset": 1, "full": true }`, each field with those defaults: two `engine_white` sprites, the back centred over the holder's drawn centre with its bottom `rise` above the drawn top (half the sprite's height times its scale, the point itself without a sprite), the fill `inset` inside it from the left at the health's share; linear colours; at the holder's depth plus 1 and 2; unturned. Hidden while dead or without health and, with `full: false`, at full health. Without `engine_white`, one warning and nothing drawn. Unsaved presentation.
- **API:** `HealthBar` (`new`, `with_size`, `with_rise`, `with_colors`, `with_inset`, `hidden_at_full`, `size`, `full`), `HealthBarPlugin`.
