## 2. Design

### 2.1 The light at a point (P7-5, Step 1)

- `tungsten_core::lighting::light_at(world, point) -> Vec3`: the sum over every entity with a `Transform` and a `Light`, as the extract takes them (A10), of what a flat white lit sprite at `point` gets from it: a point light `color · intensity · a² · h / √(d² + h²)`, `d` the distance from its `Transform`'s position, `r` its radius taken as `max(r, 1e-4)`, as the shader clamps it (`lit_sprite.wgsl:131`; `Light::point` refuses no radius, `components.rs:184-192`), `a = clamp(1 − d / r, 0, 1)`, `h = r / 2`; a directional light `color · intensity / √2`; a contribution that is not finite counts as none. No ambient (a game adds `AmbientLight` itself), no cap and no view, so a fixed-step read never follows the camera (Q9). `light_level(world, point) -> f32` is its Rec. 709 luma (0.2126, 0.7152, 0.0722): chemistry's light amount.
- A test reads the shader's point and directional lines and fails when they change, as `light_cap_is_sixteen` reads its array (`tests/lighting.rs:9-20`).

### 2.2 Statuses (P7-4's statuses, PA-8's scale and lines, Step 2)

An effect keeps its name (Q2); these optional fields make it a status, and an effect without them behaves and saves as today (A1–A3, A20).

- **Intensity:** `intensity: { "max": m, "decay": d }`, `m` finite above 0, `d` at least 0 a second. An instance holds an intensity in (0, `m`]; an application builds it by its request's amount (`EffectRequest::with_amount`, 1 by default) times the request's status effect, capped at `m`, and refreshes its time by `stack.refresh`, its stack count left at 1. The tick takes `d · min(dt, left)` off; at 0 it has run out (`EffectExpired`, as an expiry). `Effects::intensity(id)` reads it.
- **Bands:** `bands: [{ "at": x, "grant": [...] }]`, `at` strictly ascending in (0, `m`]: while the intensity is at least `at`, the band's tags are granted through the instance's ownership record and the entity's granted table, as `grant`'s are (A2), so a prior tag and two owners keep theirs. Step 3 adds a band's `enter` outputs.
- **By intensity:** an intensity status's `scale` curve reads its intensity, not the request's scale: its lines are re-placed in the step its factor changes and its payload scales at each tick; `into: duration` reads the intensity after the application's build. The curve's check (`y` at least 0, A1) stands.
- **Fuel:** `fuel: { "stat": s, "rate": r }`: while the instance lives, the holder's `s` base drains by `r` a second (`Stats::set_base`, `foundation/stats.rs:553`); at 0, or on a holder without `s`, it has run out (§5.2's fuel, the backlog's draining effect).
- **Status effect (PA-8):** `status_effect` joins the well-known stats, 1 where absent. A request's status effect is the one it names (`with_status_effect`, as Step 3's entries from a delivery do), else its source's `status_effect` at the resolve, else 1; it multiplies the request's amount and its scale. No entity today has the stat, so nothing changes.
- **Instance lines (PA-8):** `EffectRequest::with_lines([(stat, Line)])`: placed under the instance's source beside the effect's own, saved on the instance, replaced by a re-application; Step 3's `apply` output writes them as data.
- **Saved:** the instance's intensity, lines and Step 3's rate, each skipped at its default.
- **Recipes:** top-down suppression building `suppressed` through bands `cover` and `pinned` (SF14); a platformer lamp burning its `oil` (LW15).

### 2.3 Chemistry (P7-4's interactions, PA-8's rates, Step 3)

- **Where:** `crates/tungsten-kit/src/chemistry/`, a new domain (Q11). `ChemistryPlugin` joins `KitPlugins::set()` after `EffectsPlugin` (Q4); its system `chemistry` runs in Resolve after `damage_resolve` and `ailment_feed`, and `effect_resolve` gains `after_if_present(CHEMISTRY)`, so the statuses it applies land in its step. It returns before any query while the World holds no `interactions` entry, no queued entry and no tile status (A16).
- **Rules:** the `interactions` kind, a rule an entry: `{ "stimulus": "heat", "on": { "all": ["material.wood"] }, "min": 0.5, "outputs": [...] }`. `stimulus` names a damage type or a declared tag; the kit declares `stimulus.impact`, `stimulus.light` and `stimulus.blast` with its own tags (`foundation/tags.rs:35-46`, Q5). `on` is a `TagFilter` on the receiver's tags, which hold its properties and its statuses' grants and bands; `min` the least amount (0). For a stimulus and a receiver the most specific passing rule runs alone: the deepest stimulus name (an ancestor's rule takes its descendants), then the most dotted segments in `on`'s `all`, then the lowest entry ID (Q7). The load checks every tag, status, prefab and handler a rule names.
- **Stimuli (this step):** each non-periodic `DamagedPart` since the system last read: a stimulus of each of its types, its amount after the resistances (the ladder's fill, A4), from its source. Periodic parts stimulate nothing (Q6). Steps 4, 5 and 7 add emissions, impacts, light, cells and blasts.
- **The queue,** a saved resource: a stimulus joins it only when some rule names its name or an ancestor's, as one entry per stimulus and receiver. A merge sums the raw amounts (a rule's `min` reads them) and, apart, each hit's amount times its status effect (a `scale` build reads that sum), both in `f64` and clamped to `f32::MAX` (a stimulus that is no hit carries a status effect of 1), and keeps the largest status effect (a fixed `amount` build's), the largest DoT base, the least depth, the first credit and the entry's age, the run it was first queued for. Past the bound (16,384) an add is dropped and counted, a warning once a step (A15). A run takes at most the cap (256) of the entries queued before it, oldest first: by age, then depth, then the receiver's save order (`foundation/saved.rs:64`), then the stimulus, as 01's cascade runs by due run first (`cascade.rs:337-349`), so fresh work never passes a waiting entry. Each decides when it runs: the receiver's winning rule then; nothing for a receiver gone or despawning, or when no rule passes. A dead receiver still reacts, its `build` and `apply` refused as effects refuse the dead (A2) while its `remove` and `consume` run, and the kit's end-of-step despawn holds an entity due to go while the queue holds an entry queued for it before then (`lifecycle/ended.rs:88-100`); a held entity takes no new entry, so a killing hit's or a blast's reaction runs even behind the cap and the hold ends within ⌈bound / cap⌉ runs by the oldest-first order (Q18). An output that stimulates (Steps 4 and 7) queues at depth + 1, and past the depth limit (8) it is dropped and counted (`cut`), so a cycle of rules ends; a reaction's damage carries its depth onto its parts (`DamagedPart::depth`, in `DamageRequest`'s padding, A8), so a cycle through damage ends too. `ChemistryPlugin::with_cap`, `with_bound` and `with_depth` set them; the `Chemistry` resource reads `pending()`, `dropped()`, `cut()` and `ran()` (Q8).
- **Outputs,** a fixed list run in order on the receiver: `build` (a status by `amount`, or `scale` × the entry's weighted sum), `apply` (a status, with `dot` and `lines`), each naming its request's status effect (2.2): 1 for a `scale` build, whose sum carries each hit's already, and the entry's largest otherwise; `remove`, `consume` (intensity off; at 0 it has run out), `damage` (typed parts on the receiver from the credit, queued for the next step's resolve), `spawn` (a prefab at the receiver's point, from it, A14) and `handler` (a game's Rust handler by name, `ChemistryPlugin::with_handler`; the load refuses an unregistered name). A status gains `on_end` (outputs when an instance ends: run out, decayed, its fuel spent, removed or ended) and a band's `enter` (outputs when the intensity first reaches it from below), queued as a rule's are: at depth 0 from a tick, at the entry's depth + 1 from an entry. Steps 4–7 add `emit`, `set_tile`, `become` and `blast`.
- **PA-8's rates:** `DamagedPart` gains `dot_base` and `status_effect`: for a sheet's part (the ARPG design's §6.4, step 7) its drawn amount before the factors and the crit, times its `delivery.dot` sums with their deferred lines (A9), times the hit's status effect, which the sheet snapshots at use; for a plain part its amount before the factors, times its source's `status_effect`. `apply`'s `dot: s` fixes the instance's rate at `s × dot_base` a second; its ticks deal `rate × period`, shared over the effect's damage types in their data's proportions and typed at the application: they skip the source's live conversion (`pipeline.rs:1542-1546`), which today's payloads keep, by a flag beside the depth in `DamageRequest`'s padding, both saved with the damage queue and skipped at their defaults; the rate carries its hit's status effect in its DoT base and is not scaled again; a re-application's rate replaces it, as its factor does (Q15).
- **Trace:** each output run writes a `KitTrace` event naming its rule (Q13).
- **Recipes:** top-down fire on wooden crates building `burning` and cold on `status.burning` removing it; a platformer torch whose hit ignites by its rate. Their rules' filters name tags only the new prefabs carry, so the pin's script meets none.

### 2.4 Spread, impacts and light (Step 4)

- **Emissions:** `emit: { "stimulus": s, "amount": a, "radius": r, "period": p }` on a status: every `p` seconds of an instance's life, counted as its period is (A3), its holder emits `s` of `a` times its factor at its point to every receiver within `r` but itself, at depth 0; the `emit` output does the same from an entry at its depth + 1. The tick hands emissions to chemistry in a kept buffer, adding no query.
- **Receivers in a radius:** each entity with `Tags` and a point (a body's `Position`, else its `Transform`), within `r` by a body's surface (`Shape::distance`, as A11) or its point. A grid of 64 px cells, built from kept buffers only at a run with radius work; the blast uses it too (Q16).
- **A tick:** `chemistry_tick` joins Tick after `effect_tick` and `ailment_tick`, idle as `chemistry` is: it registers the impacts' queue and samples the light, and Steps 5 and 6 tick tile statuses and `Became` there; kit.rs's snapshot and edges and the template's `SCHEDULE` line take its name.
- **Impacts:** with a rule loaded whose stimulus is `stimulus.impact` or an ancestor of it, as rules match (2.3), `chemistry_tick` registers `ContactBegan` before the step's physics, since the record runs only while a queue exists at the step's start (`D-233`, A12), so a world's first step counts: each begin stimulates both bodies, its amount the closing speed, its credit the other's `Source` or the other. A world without such a rule keeps the record off.
