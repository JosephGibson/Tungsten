### 2.3 The kit's caps for a game's spawns (Step 1)

- **At once.** `admit_spawn(world, prepared, source) -> Option<Admission>`, public: a prefab the game spawns at once (`PreparedPrefab::spawn`), admitted under its prefab's cap as a kit route admits (a leaving source refused, the group counted, `past` applied): its victims are listed to end, each with `culled` when named, and come back oldest first in `Admission::culled`, for the game to end at once with its own look or leave to the kit's end; the prefab comes back stamped. It is live once spawned, so it is not counted as queued for the flush. An uncapped prefab comes back as it was with no victims; a refused one is `None`, ending nothing. `Admission { prepared, culled }` is `#[non_exhaustive]` with public fields. A victim the game despawns at once leaves the kit's end quietly: its despawn skipped with no despawn trace and no warning; a `culled` it had named is skipped as any gone subject's cue is, with the cue runner's skip trace (01 names none).
- **Before a spend.** `cap_refuses(world, prefab) -> bool`: whether a spawn of `prefab` now meets a full `refuse` cap, this frame's queued admissions counted, for a game that checks before it spends, in its own order.
- **Through a load.** An admission's stamp is saved as `cap_admitted`, a component with an empty form, restored as the earliest stamp: a restored admitted holder counts, ordered with the restored by the kit's save order, before this run's admissions. A holder with neither the mark nor a `Source`, as the map's, stays aside after a load as before it.
- **The dead.** `cap`'s `count_dead` (`Cap::without_dead`, `Cap::counts_dead`), true by default as today: false leaves a holder whose `Health` is dead out of the count and out of the victims (Q5).
- **Kept as it is:** a use's spawn payload refused at its cap after the spend, as `D-245` has it (Q6); 01 checks its refusing caps first through `cap_refuses`, keeping its refusals' order (A6).
- The `cap` form takes `count_dead` (a flag, so the numeric census holds); `api/`, `DESIGN.md`'s caps sentence and the describe snapshot's `cap` lines move.
